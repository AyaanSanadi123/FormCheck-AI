# DEADLIFT GATEKEEPER: OBJECTIVES & LOGIC
# Version: 1.0

## 1. THE PROBLEM: "THE WEDGE" SETUP
Unlike Squat (Standing) or Bench (Lying), the Deadlift starts in a specific "Crouch" position known as "The Wedge."
* **Geometry:** The lifter forms a triangle with the floor.
* **Hierarchy:** Shoulders must be higher than Hips, and Hips must be higher than Knees.
* **Verticality:** The Shins must be near-vertical (80-100 degrees) to ensure the bar is over the mid-foot.

## 2. THE ORIENTATION CHALLENGE
Users can face Left (-90°) or Right (+90°).
* **Issue:** The logic for "Bar Drift" (drift away from legs) depends entirely on which way the user is facing.
* **Solution:** The Gatekeeper calculates the `facing_direction` (-1 for Left, +1 for Right) during the static hold and passes this to the Normalizer. This allows the Normalizer to mathematically "flip" Left-facing users so the Rep Logic only ever has to solve for one direction.

## 3. THE VALIDATION CRITERIA
The Gatekeeper will only issue a "GO" signal if:
1.  **Visibility:** Feet, Knees, Hips, Shoulders, and Wrists are all visible (Full Kinetic Chain).
2.  **Triangle Hierarchy:** `Shoulder.Y < Hip.Y < Knee.Y` (Remember Y is inverted/down).
3.  **Arm Verticality:** The horizontal distance between Shoulder and Wrist is minimal (Arms hanging straight down).
4.  **Stability:** The user holds this "Wedge" position for 1.5 seconds (45 frames) with low variance.

## 4. OUTPUT (THE PASSPORT)
* `floor_y`: The Y-coordinate of the Heels (Zero line).
* `torso_length`: Distance between Shoulder and Hip (Scale factor).
* `facing_side`: -1 (Left) or +1 (Right).
