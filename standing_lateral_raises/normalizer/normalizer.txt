LATERAL RAISE NORMALIZER: GEOMETRY STRATEGY
# Version: 1.3 (Mid-Shoulder Pivot & Y-Lock)
================================================================

1. THE CORE OBJECTIVE: "SQUARING THE FRONTAL PLANE"
--------------------------------------------------
In a frontal-view exercise like the Lateral Raise, perspective 
distortion is the primary cause of false "Asymmetry" flags. If a 
user stands at a slight angle (e.g., 10° off-center), the arm 
closer to the camera appears longer and higher in 2D space.

The Normalizer creates a "Virtual Camera" that is always perfectly 
perpendicular to the user's collarbone.

2. REFINED ROTATION LOGIC
-------------------------

A. Mid-Shoulder Pivot Point (The "Anchor")
- STRATEGY: Explicitly set the pivot of rotation to the midpoint 
  between Left (11) and Right (12) shoulders.
- RATIONALE: The lateral raise is a shoulder-centric movement. 
  Anchoring the rotation here ensures that the "Torso Vector" 
  and "Arm Vectors" remain concentric, preventing "pendulum 
  swing" artifacts that occur if the pivot is at the feet or hips.



B. X-Z Plane Rectification
- STRATEGY: Calculate the 'Facing Angle' (Theta) using the 
  Z-depth difference between shoulders.
- ACTION: Apply an inverse rotation matrix to the X-Z (Floor) 
  plane until the Facing Angle is 0.0°.
- BENEFIT: Eliminates "Depth-shortening" where the 2D projection 
  of a limb appears shorter because it is pointed toward or 
  away from the lens.

3. DATA INTEGRITY: THE "Y-AXIS LOCK"
------------------------------------
- CONSTRAINT: The Y-coordinate (Vertical/Gravity) is strictly 
  excluded from the rotation matrix.
- IMPACT: Because Y remains raw, the 'torso_baseline' (Shoulder-to-Hip 
  vertical distance) captured by the Gatekeeper remains 
  mathematically constant.
- RESULT: This prevents the Normalizer from accidentally 
  triggering "Shrug" faults through perspective rotation.

4. TEMPORAL STABILIZATION
-------------------------
- METHOD: Exponential Moving Average (EMA) applied to the 
  Correction Delta (the rotation angle).
- ALPHA: 0.25 (Balances responsiveness with stability).
- IMPACT: Prevents the entire skeleton from "shaking" due to 
  minor Z-depth sensor flicker, providing a smooth feed for 
  velocity calculations in the Rep Analyzer.



NOTE: Version 1.3 ensures that the output landmarks are "Analysis-Ready." 
By squaring the user, we ensure that:
(Left_Wrist_X - Left_Sh_X) == (Right_Wrist_X - Right_Sh_X) 
This makes symmetry detection a simple linear comparison.
