LATERAL RAISE VISUALIZER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
# Version: 1.1 (Symmetry & Proportional HUD)
================================================================

1. THE CORE PROBLEM: "HIDDEN ASYMMETRY"
---------------------------------------
In a Lateral Raise, a user often believes their arms are level when 
one is actually 10-15° lower. Traditional video doesn't highlight 
this clearly. The Visualizer uses the Rectified Frontal Plane from 
the Normalizer to make these micro-deviations visible.

2. THE "SYMMETRY LEVELER" (Shoulder Bar)
----------------------------------------
- CONCEPT: A dynamic horizontal bar connecting the Left and Right 
  acromion processes (Shoulder points).
- GOAL: To provide a "Spirit Level" for the upper body.
- VISUAL LOGIC:
    * WHITE: Shoulders are stable and horizontal.
    * YELLOW: Asymmetry detected (One arm lagging).
    * RED: Shrugging detected (Torso compression > 10%).



3. THE "WING ARCS" (Abduction Tracers)
--------------------------------------
- CONCEPT: Trailing comet-tails following the wrists.
- GOAL: To visualize the "Path of Scaption."
- VISUAL LOGIC:
    * CYAN PATH: Standard movement arc.
    * THICKNESS: The path is thicker at the wrist and tapers off, 
      helping the user visualize the "width" of their raise.
    * SYMMETRY CHECK: If the arcs are not concentric, the user 
      can instantly see which side is losing range of motion.



4. PROPORTIONAL HUD ELEMENTS
----------------------------
- ABDUCTION GAUGE: Displays the average angle (calculated via 
  Vector Dot Product) at the center of the shoulder leveler.
- MOMENTUM GLOW: If "BODY_SWAY" is flagged by the analyzer, the 
  Hip-to-Ankle segments of the skeleton glow RED. This signals 
  that the legs/hips are providing "cheated" momentum.

5. FEEDBACK TOASTS (Coach's Voice)
----------------------------------
- "REACH SHOULDER HEIGHT": Triggered by the Shallow-Rep flag.
- "KEEP SHOULDERS DOWN": Triggered by the Shrug-Ratio flag.
- "STOP THE BODY SWING": Triggered by the Sway-Distance flag.



NOTE: By using the Normalized landmarks, the Visualizer remains 
"Perspective Pure." Even if the user is standing at a slight 
angle to the lens, the AR overlays will appear perfectly squared 
to their body.
