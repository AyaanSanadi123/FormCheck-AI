LATERAL RAISE GATEKEEPER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
Version: 1.2 (Anatomical Scaling Refinement)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
The Gatekeeper establishes a "Body-Relative" baseline. By calculating 
anatomical proportions (Torso and Arm lengths) instead of fixed pixel 
coordinates, the system remains accurate regardless of camera distance, 
user height, or slight forward leaning.

2. THE VALIDATION PIPELINE
--------------------------

A. Frame-Level Rejection (Instant)
- VISIBILITY: Shoulders, Hips, and Wrists must have confidence > 0.90.
- PERSPECTIVE: Z-depth difference between Left/Right shoulders must 
  be < 0.15. This ensures the "Anatomical Passport" isn't distorted 
  by a rotated torso.
- INITIAL POSTURE: Wrists must be vertically below the Hips. This 
  ensures the "Resting Torso Length" is captured without deltoid 
  contraction.



B. Temporal Validation (Stability Window)
- WINDOW SIZE: 45 Frames (~1.5 seconds).
- REQUIREMENT: The Euclidean distance between the Shoulder and Hip 
  must remain stable. If the user moves erratically, the buffer 
  flushes to prevent a "noisy" baseline.

3. THE CALIBRATION PASSPORT (Data Latch)
----------------------------------------
Upon successful validation, the following anatomical ratios are locked:

* torso_baseline: The Euclidean distance (L^2 norm) from the Shoulder 
  to the Hip. This serves as the "Vertical Anchor." 
  - USE: If this distance decreases during a rep, it indicates the 
    Shoulder is moving toward the Ear (SHRUGGING).
* arm_length: The cumulative length of the arm segments. 
  - USE: Used to calculate the radius of the abduction arc and 
    normalize the 90-degree peak.
* neutral_hip_x: The horizontal center-point of the pelvis.
  - USE: Used to detect torso sway or momentum-cheating.



4. USER FEEDBACK STRATEGY
-------------------------
* "Ensure your full body is visible": Triggered by landmark dropout.
* "Face the camera directly": Triggered by Z-axis asymmetry.
* "Analyzing Posture...": Visual indicator of the 45-frame window.

NOTE: Version 1.2 moves away from "sh_y_baseline" as a primary check. 
By using "torso_baseline," the system can differentiate between a 
user who is leaning forward (constant torso length) and a user who 
is shrugging (shortened torso-to-ear distance).
