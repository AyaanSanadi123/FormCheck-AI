LATERAL RAISE REP LOGIC: STRATEGY & SCORING
# Version: 1.2 (Vector-Based Refinement)
================================================================

1. CORE METRICS
---------------
* Anatomical Abduction: The angle between the "Spine Vector" (Hip-to-
  Shoulder) and the "Arm Vector" (Shoulder-to-Wrist).
* Torso Ratio: The real-time Euclidean distance between shoulder 
  and hip compared against the 'torso_baseline'.
* Hip Drift: Horizontal (X) displacement of the pelvic center.
* Angular Symmetry: The variance between Left and Right abduction.

2. REFINED SCORING RULES (Start at 100)
---------------------------------------

Major Deductions (-15)
1. "SHRUGGING": Detected when the Torso Ratio drops below 0.90 
   (10% compression). This signifies the AC joint is moving toward 
   the ear, engaging the Upper Traps over the Medial Deltoid.
2. "SHALLOW": Peak abduction fails to reach 80°. Incomplete range 
   of motion results in sub-optimal muscle fiber recruitment.
3. "BODY_SWAY": Hip center deviates > 8% of total torso length. 
   Indicates momentum-cheating via a "hip-flick."

Minor Deductions (-10)
1. "ASYMMETRY": A difference of > 15° between Left and Right arm 
   elevation. Indicates neural drive imbalance or strength deficit.
2. "CONTROL": Rapid downward velocity (Y-axis drop) suggesting 
   gravity is doing the work instead of an eccentric contraction.



3. VECTOR STATE MACHINE
-----------------------
* ASCENT: Triggered when (Arm_Vector · Spine_Vector) indicates 
  an angle > 20° with negative Y-velocity.
* PEAK: The point of maximum abduction where Y-velocity crosses 
  zero. The "Shallow" check is performed here.
* DESCENT: Positive Y-velocity (weights returning to floor).
* COMPLETE: Angle returns to < 25° relative to the spine.

4. DATA INPUTS
--------------
* gatekeeper.calibration: Provides 'torso_baseline' for the ratio 
  denominator and 'neutral_hip_x' for sway monitoring.
* normalizer.landmarks: Provides the X-Z rectified coordinates 
  ensuring the Dot Product calculation is perspective-pure.



NOTE: Version 1.2 uses the Dot Product to allow for "Scaption." If 
the user raises dumbbells 30° in front of the frontal plane (the 
natural plane of the scapula), the angle remains anatomically 
accurate, preventing the "False Shallow" errors of 2D slope math.
