# BENCH PRESS GATEKEEPER: OBJECTIVES & LOGIC
# Version: 1.0

## 1. THE PROBLEM: OCCLUSION & HORIZONTAL ALIGNMENT
Unlike the Squat, the Bench Press occurs in a horizontal plane. This introduces two major risks for the Logic Engine:
1.  **Plate Occlusion:** If the camera is too close to the head or feet (0° or 180°), the weight plates hide the Elbow and Wrist joints.
2.  **Bench Occlusion:** The bench itself can hide the Hips or Shoulders if the angle is too low.

## 2. THE SOLUTION: THE "GOLDEN VIEW" ENFORCEMENT
The Gatekeeper restricts the user to a side-diagonal view (60° to 120°) on either the Left or Right side. 

### Validation Criteria:
* **Horizontal Body Check:** The angle between the Shoulder-Midpoint and Hip-Midpoint must be near-horizontal (e.g., within 20° of the X-axis).
* **Visibility Threshold:** 100% visibility (score > 0.5) is required for:
    * Primary: Wrists, Elbows, Shoulders (The Pressing Unit).
    * Secondary: Hips (To detect "Bridging" / Lifting off the bench).
    * Tertiary: Feet (To detect "Heel Drive" stability).
* **Facing Angle:** Enforces a range of 60° - 120° relative to the bench to ensure the Normalizer can accurately reconstruct the side-profile.

## 3. STATE TRANSITION
* **PENDING:** Searching for a horizontal human.
* **CALIBRATING:** User is lying on the bench; system is collecting floor/bench baselines.
* **SUCCESS:** "Green Light" given to the SquatRep logic.
