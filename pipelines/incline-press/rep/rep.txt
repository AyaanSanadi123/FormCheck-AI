# INCLINE PRESS REP LOGIC: DETAILED PHYSICS & SCORING REPORT
# Version: 1.0
# Blueprint Standard: Compliant

## 1. THE GEOMETRIC ENGINE: "THE VERTICAL PRESS"
Because the Normalizer locked the Shoulder to (0,0) and inverted the Y-axis so UP is positive:
* **The Trajectory:** The user is pushing the weight up into the `+Y` quadrant.
* **The Forearm Vector:** To efficiently transfer force and protect the shoulder, the Wrist should remain vertically stacked over the Elbow (meaning `Wrist.x` should be very close to `Elbow.x`).

## 2. THE REP CYCLE ("DOWN-FIRST" STATE MACHINE)
The user starts with the weight pressed up and lowers it to their chest. 

### State 1: IDLE (Lockout)
* **Goal:** Arms fully extended upward.
* **Trigger to ECCENTRIC:** Elbow angle drops rapidly (angular velocity < -15.0 deg/sec) and falls below 160°.

### State 2: ECCENTRIC (The Drop)
* **Goal:** Lower the bar/dumbbells to the chest under control.
* **Peak Detection:** We track `min_elbow_angle` to ensure they hit proper depth.
* **Trigger to CONCENTRIC:** The angular velocity crosses zero and becomes positive (the weight is being pushed back up).

### State 3: CONCENTRIC (The Push)
* **Goal:** Push the weight back to lockout.
* **Completion:** The elbow angle opens back up > 150°, signaling a return to the IDLE state.

## 3. FAULT DETECTION MATRIX & SCORING HIERARCHY

### Severe Deductions (-10 Points)
**1. UNSTACKED_JOINTS (Dangerous Forearm Angle)**
* **The Problem:** The user lets their wrists fall backward toward their head, or drift too far forward, placing massive shearing force on the elbow and shoulder joints.
* **The Math:** We calculate the horizontal distance between `Wrist.x` and `Elbow.x`. If `abs(Wrist.x - Elbow.x) > 0.15` Torso Units during the press, they lose the vertical stack.

**2. HIP_BRIDGE (Turning Incline into Flat Bench)**
* **The Problem:** The user lifts their butt off the bench to cheat, shifting the load from the upper chest to the lower chest.
* **The Math:** We track the live `current_torso_angle`. If it drops more than 15° below their `setup_torso_angle` (meaning they flattened out), we flag it.

### Minor Deductions (-5 Points)
**1. SHALLOW_REP (Half-Rep)**
* **The Problem:** Not lowering the weight far enough to stretch the pecs.
* **The Math:** Evaluated at the transition from ECCENTRIC to CONCENTRIC. If `min_elbow_angle > 90.0°`, they shorted the depth.

**2. SHORT_LOCKOUT (Skipping the Top)**
* **The Problem:** Starting the next rep before fully locking out.
* **The Math:** If a new ECCENTRIC phase begins before the elbow angle recovers to at least 150°.
