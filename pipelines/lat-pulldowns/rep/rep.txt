# LAT PULLDOWN REP LOGIC: STRATEGY & SCORING
# Version: 1.0

## 1. DYNAMIC ROM (Range of Motion) TRACKING
* **The "Zero Point":** Arms fully extended (Normalized Y = 0).
* **The "Target Line":** The resting Y-coordinate of the Shoulders.
* **The ROM Scale:** We define the distance from Zero to Target as "100% ROM".

## 2. THE REP ZONES (At the Bottom of the Pull)
When the user finishes the pull (velocity hits 0 and reverses), we check their `Max_Depth`:
* **< 50% ROM:** Ignored (System resets silently, no "Rep Failed" penalty).
* **50% - 85% ROM:** FLAG as "PARTIAL_REP" (Half-repping, missed the squeeze).
* **> 85% ROM:** "Good Rep" (Accounts for fatigue/clavicle height).

## 3. FAULT HIERARCHY

### Severe (-10 Points)
1.  **"ASYMMETRIC_PULL":** `abs(Left_Wrist.y - Right_Wrist.y) > 0.15` (One side pulling significantly more weight).
2.  **"MOMENTUM_SWING":** Leaning back to use body weight. Measured by 2D foreshortening: if the vertical distance between Shoulders and Hips shrinks by > 15%, they are leaning back too far.

### Minor (-5 Points)
1.  **"PARTIAL_REP":** Stopping short of 85% ROM.
2.  **"SHRUGGING":** Shoulders elevating (moving towards Y=0) during the concentric pull, indicating trap-dominance over lats.
