# CABLE TRICEP KICKBACK REP LOGIC: STRATEGY & SCORING
# Version: 1.0
# Blueprint Standard: Compliant

## 1. THE GEOMETRIC ENGINE: "THE FLOATING HINGE"
Because the user is bent over, their elbow is suspended in mid-air. To isolate the triceps, the upper arm must act as a perfectly rigid, floating hinge.
* **The Anchor:** The Elbow must remain locked at its baseline (X, Y) coordinate relative to the Shoulder `(0,0)`.
* **The Lever:** The Forearm pushes backward into the negative X space (`-X`).

## 2. THE REP CYCLE ("CONCENTRIC-FIRST" STATE MACHINE)
The user begins the lift from a flexed position, fighting the cable tension immediately.

### State 1: IDLE (Flexed/Ready)
* **Goal:** Arm bent, waiting to extend.
* **Trigger to CONCENTRIC:** The elbow angle increases rapidly (positive angular velocity).

### State 2: CONCENTRIC (The Kickback)
* **Goal:** Extend the forearm straight back until parallel with the upper arm.
* **Peak Detection:** We track the maximum extension achieved (`max_elbow_angle`).
* **Trigger to ECCENTRIC:** The angular velocity crosses zero and becomes negative (the arm begins to bend again).

### State 3: ECCENTRIC (The Return)
* **Goal:** Resist the cable tension as the arm returns to the flexed starting position.
* **Completion:** The elbow angle drops back down (e.g., < 110°), signaling a return to the IDLE state.

## 3. FAULT DETECTION MATRIX & SCORING HIERARCHY

### Severe Deductions (-10 Points)
**1. PENDULUM_SWING (Breaking the Hinge)**
* **The Problem:** The user drops their elbow forward on the eccentric and swings it back on the concentric to create momentum.
* **The Math:** We calculate the Euclidean distance between the live `Elbow(x,y)` and the normalized `baseline_el(x,y)`. If this drift exceeds `0.20` Arm Units, the hinge is broken.

**2. TORSO_BOB (Heaving the Back)**
* **The Problem:** Standing up and violently dropping the torso to heave the weight back.
* **The Math:** We compare the live `current_torso_angle` to the `setup_torso_angle`. A deviation of > 15° triggers the fault.

### Minor Deductions (-5 Points)
**1. SHORT_LOCKOUT (Missing the Squeeze)**
* **The Problem:** Reversing the rep before the arm is fully straight.
* **The Math:** Evaluated at the transition to ECCENTRIC. If `max_elbow_angle < 160.0°`, they shorted the peak contraction.
