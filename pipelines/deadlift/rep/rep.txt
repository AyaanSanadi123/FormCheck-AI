# DEADLIFT REP LOGIC: STRATEGY & SCORING
# Version: 1.0

## 1. CORE METRICS (Normalized Space)
* **Start Position:** We capture `Hip.y` and `Shoulder.y` at the exact moment of Liftoff.
* **Vertical Delta:** `Current_Y - Start_Y`.
* **Torso Length:** Used to normalize drift distances.

## 2. THE "STRIPPER PULL" FORMULA
The most critical check happens in the "First Pull" (0% to 40% height).
* IF `Delta_Hip` > (`Delta_Shoulder` + Threshold):
    * The hips are moving, but the shoulders are not.
    * RESULT: The back angle is becoming horizontal.
    * FAULT: Severe (-10).

## 3. THE "BAR DRIFT" FORMULA
The Normalizer sets the Ankles at X=0.
* Ideally, `Bar.x` stays near 0 (Vertical path).
* If `Bar.x` becomes positive (moving forward away from shins), leverage is lost.

## 4. STATE MACHINE TRIGGERS
* **IDLE**: `Bar.y` (Height) < 0.05.
* **LIFTOFF**: `Bar.y` > 0.05. Capture Start Coordinates.
* **LOCKOUT**: `Velocity` approx 0 AND `Bar.y` > 0.7 (High enough).
* **COMPLETE**: `Bar.y` < 0.05 (Back on floor).
