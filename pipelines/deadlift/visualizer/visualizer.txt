# DEADLIFT VISUALIZER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
# Version: 1.0

## 1. THE "PLUMB LINE" (GRAVITY REFERENCE)
* **Concept:** A deadlift fights gravity directly. The most efficient path is a straight vertical line over the mid-foot.
* **Visual:** Draw a vertical dashed line starting from the Ankle (X=0 in normalized space) extending upwards.
* **Feedback:** "Keep the bar on the dotted line."

## 2. THE SPINE TRACKER (STRIPPER PULL VISUALIZATION)
* **Concept:** Users can't feel when their hips shoot up too fast.
* **Visual:** Draw a thick line connecting Shoulder to Hip.
* **Logic:**
    * **Green:** Normal ascent.
    * **Red:** If "STRIPPER_PULL" fault is active. This gives immediate feedback on *why* the rep was bad ("Oh, my back line turned red at the bottom").

## 3. THE BAR TRACER
* **Concept:** Trailing history of the wrist position.
* **Color Coding:**
    * **Cyan/Green:** Vertical path.
    * **Red:** Horizontal drift > Threshold.

## 4. HUD ELEMENTS
* **State:** (IDLE, PULL, LOCKOUT, DROP).
* **Feedback:** Large text toast (e.g., "SQUEEZE GLUTES!").
