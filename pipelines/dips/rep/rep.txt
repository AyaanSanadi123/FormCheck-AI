# DIPS REP LOGIC: DETAILED PHYSICS & SCORING REPORT
# Version: 1.0
# Blueprint Standard: Compliant

## 1. THE GEOMETRIC ENGINE: "THE RELATIVE DROP"
Because the Normalizer locked the Shoulder to (0,0) and inverted the Y-axis (UP is +Y):
* **The Starting Position:** The Wrist sits far below the shoulder in the `-Y` space. The Elbow is locked out.
* **The Eccentric Drop:** As the user drops, the distance between the Shoulder and Wrist shrinks. Geometrically, the Wrist and Elbow travel UP toward the `(0,0)` origin.
* **The Turnaround (Depth):** A true dip requires the shoulder to drop below the elbow. In our normalized space, this means the Elbow's Y-coordinate must physically cross the `0` threshold and become positive (`Elbow.y > 0`). 

## 2. THE REP CYCLE ("DOWN-FIRST" STATE MACHINE)
The user begins suspended in the air.

### State 1: IDLE (Lockout)
* **Goal:** Arms fully extended, supporting body weight.
* **Trigger to ECCENTRIC:** The elbow angle decreases rapidly (angular velocity < -10.0 deg/sec) and drops below 160°.

### State 2: ECCENTRIC (The Drop)
* **Goal:** Lower the body until the shoulders cross the elbows.
* **Peak Detection:** We track the minimum elbow angle (`min_elbow_angle`) and the maximum Y-height of the elbow (`max_elbow_y`).
* **Trigger to CONCENTRIC:** The angular velocity flips from negative to positive (the body is being pushed back up).

### State 3: CONCENTRIC (The Push)
* **Goal:** Push back up to full suspension.
* **Completion:** The elbow angle opens back up > 155°, returning to IDLE.

## 3. FAULT DETECTION MATRIX & SCORING HIERARCHY

### Severe Deductions (-10 Points)
**1. PENDULUM_SWING (Kipping the Legs)**
* **The Problem:** The user swings their legs/hips violently forward and backward to generate momentum and cheat the concentric push.
* **The Math:** Since Torso Length is perfectly scaled to `1.0`, we track the Hip's horizontal `X` coordinate. If the Hip drifts horizontally by more than `0.20` Torso Units from its starting position during the rep, they are swinging.

### Minor Deductions (-5 Points)
**1. SHALLOW_REP (Ego Dip)**
* **The Problem:** Reversing the movement before hitting 90 degrees or crossing the shoulder.
* **The Math:** Evaluated at the transition to CONCENTRIC. If the `min_elbow_angle > 90.0°` OR `max_elbow_y < 0.0` (meaning the elbow never reached the shoulder origin level), it was a half-rep.

**2. SHORT_LOCKOUT (Constant Tension Exploit)**
* **The Problem:** Skipping the hardest part of the dip by never fully straightening the arms at the top.
* **The Math:** If a new ECCENTRIC drop begins before the elbow angle recovers to at least 155°.
