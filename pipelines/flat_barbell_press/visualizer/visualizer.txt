# BENCH PRESS VISUALIZER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
# Version: 1.0

## 1. THE PROBLEM: "INVISIBLE PHYSICS"
In a Squat, the user can see if they are deep enough. In a Bench Press, the user cannot see two critical things:
1.  **Bar Path:** They cannot see the "J-Curve" trajectory while lying down. They often press in a straight vertical line (Guillotine Press) without knowing it.
2.  **Elbow Flare:** They cannot see if their elbows are drifting past 80° relative to their torso.

## 2. THE SOLUTION: THE "FIGHTER PILOT" HUD
The Visualizer creates an Augmented Reality overlay that reveals these hidden metrics.

### A. The Bar Path Tracer (The "Comet Tail")
* **Concept:** We draw a trailing line behind the bar (wrists) for the last 15-30 frames.
* **Goal:** To show the *shape* of the movement.
* **Visual Logic:**
    * **Curve (J-Shape):** The natural, safe path.
    * **Vertical Line:** The dangerous "Guillotine" path (Flagged Red).

### B. The Elbow Flare Triangle (The "Danger Zone")
* **Concept:** We draw a filled triangle connecting the **Shoulder**, **Elbow**, and **Hip**.
* **Goal:** To visualize the "Armpit Gap."
* **Visual Logic:**
    * **Green/Transparent:** Elbows are tucked (< 75°). Safe.
    * **Solid Red:** Elbows are flared (> 85°). Dangerous. This gives immediate peripheral feedback to the user ("I see Red under my arms -> Tuck elbows").

### C. The Target Box (The "Rack" Position)
* **Concept:** A semi-transparent target drawn over the **Shoulders**.
* **Goal:** To guide the user's ascent.
* **Instruction:** "Push the bar back into the Blue Box." This naturally forces the J-Curve without explaining physics.

## 3. DATA LAYERING
1.  **Skeleton Layer:** Draws lines between raw joints (Shoulders, Elbows, Wrists) so the user knows tracking is working.
2.  **Data Layer:** Overlay the Tracer and Triangle on top of the video feed.
3.  **Feedback Layer:** Text "Toast" messages (e.g., "TUCK ELBOWS!") centered on screen.
