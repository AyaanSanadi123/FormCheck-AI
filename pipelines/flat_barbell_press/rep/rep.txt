# BENCH PRESS REP LOGIC: STRATEGY & SCORING
# Version: 1.0

## 1. CORE METRICS
* **Vertical ROM:** % of Arm Length traveled. (Must verify lock-out).
* **Horizontal Path:** The "Bar Drift" from Shoulder to Sternum.
* **Stability:** Hip height variance and Foot variance.

## 2. SCORING RULES (Start at 100)

### Major Deductions (-10)
1.  **"GLUTE_BRIDGE"**: Hip Y-coord rises > 10% of torso length above baseline.
2.  **"ASYMMETRY"**: Left/Right Wrist Y-diff > 0.05 (Normalized).
3.  **"UNSTABLE_FEET"**: Heel velocity > threshold during press.

### Minor Deductions (-5)
1.  **"BOUNCE"**: Instantaneous velocity flip at bottom > threshold.
2.  **"FLARE"**: Upper Arm vs Torso Angle > 80 degrees.
3.  **"BAD_PATH"**: Bar travels vertically (No horizontal component) OR Bar travels over face.
4.  **"SHALLOW"**: Bar does not reach within 15% of chest depth.

## 3. STATE MACHINE TRIGGERS
* **START_REP**: Arms Extended (Angle > 160°) + Bar is Stable.
* **DESCENDING**: Bar Velocity < 0.
* **BOTTOM**: Velocity passes through 0 + Height is at local minimum.
* **ASCENDING**: Bar Velocity > 0.
* **END_REP**: Arms Extended (Angle > 160°) + Velocity ≈ 0.

## 4. DATA INPUTS
* `gatekeeper.calibration`: Contains `bench_y` (Shoulder Height) and `arm_length`.
* `normalizer.process`: Returns `aligned_landmarks` (Shoulder-Anchored).
