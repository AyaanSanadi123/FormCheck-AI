================================================================
PUSH-UP VISUALIZER: PLANK PERFORMANCE STANDARDS
Version: 1.1 (Biomechanical Feedback UI)
================================================================

1. THE "PLANK RAIL" GUIDE
-------------------------
A grey guide-line connects the Shoulder to the Ankle. 
The active skeleton (Shoulder-Hip-Ankle) turns RED if the Hip 
landmark deviates significantly from this line, indicating 
a SAG or PIKE.

2. DEPTH MONITORING
-------------------
A small horizontal plane is rendered under the Wrist. 
The distance between the Shoulder (Torso line) and this 
plane represents the "Push-Up Depth."



3. FAULT HIGHLIGHTING
---------------------
* Elbow Flare: The upper arm segment (Shoulder-Elbow) turns 
  RED if the humerus-to-torso angle is > 60°.
* Head Drop: The neck segment (Ear-Shoulder) turns RED if 
  the head drops below the plank baseline.

4. DYNAMIC HUD
--------------
Displays Reps, real-time quality Score, and current State 
(e.g., ECCENTRIC vs CONCENTRIC).
