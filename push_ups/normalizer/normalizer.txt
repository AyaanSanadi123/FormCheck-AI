PUSH-UP NORMALIZER: PLANK-ALIGNED STANDARDS
Version: 1.1 (Spinal-Horizontal Canonical Pose)
================================================================

1. THE "HORIZONTAL PLANK" TRANSFORMATION
-----------------------------------------
Unlike basic normalization, this version rotates the skeletal 
frame based on the Shoulder-Hip vector. 
RESULT: The user's spine is always mathematically "flat" (aligned 
to X), regardless of whether they are doing push-ups on a 
decline, incline, or if the camera is tilted.

2. COORDINATE MAPPING
---------------------
* Hip: Always (0, 0) - The center of the plank rotation.
* Shoulder: Always fixed at (1.0, 0) in a perfect plank.
* Ankle: Should stay near (-1.3, 0).
* Floor: Represented as the Y-depth reached by the hands.

3. WHY THIS IS "GOLD STANDARD"
------------------------------
A. Hip Sag Detection: If the Hip is (0,0) and the Shoulder is (1.0, 0), 
   an Ankle with a negative Y-value indicates 'Piking' (hips up), 
   while a positive Y-value indicates 'Sagging' (hips down).
B. Depth Accuracy: Since the spine is at Y=0, the vertical travel 
   of the hands/elbows (Y-axis) perfectly measures push-up depth.
