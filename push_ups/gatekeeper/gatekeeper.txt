================================================================
PUSH-UP GATEKEEPER: SIDE-PROFILE STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Plank-Stability Calibration)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Ensures a strict side-profile orientation to monitor spinal 
alignment (Shoulder-Hip-Ankle) and vertical depth.

2. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS (check)
----------------------------------
A. Visibility:
- Monitors the 'active' side (left or right). All 5 major 
  pivots must be > 85% visible.

B. Aspect Ratio Check:
- Verifies that the horizontal distance between shoulders is 
  small relative to torso length, confirming a side profile.

C. Plank Alignment:
- Validates the user is in a 'High Plank' by checking if the 
  Shoulder-Hip-Ankle angle is > 160 degrees.

3. CALIBRATION OUTPUTS
----------------------
* active_side: The side facing the camera (LEFT/RIGHT).
* scale_factor: Biomechanical length of the torso (Sh-Hip).
* floor_y_baseline: Estimates the floor plane based on the 
  initial wrist position in the high plank.
