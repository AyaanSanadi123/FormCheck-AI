PUSH-UP REP LOGIC: PLANK STANDARDS
Version: 1.1 (Spinal Integrity Focus)
================================================================

1. PRIMARY MECHANICAL GOAL
--------------------------
To move the chest (Shoulder-Hip line) toward the fixed point of 
the hands while maintaining a 180° Shoulder-Hip-Ankle alignment.

2. STATE MACHINE (Down-First)
---------------------------
* IDLE: High plank, arms locked.
* ECCENTRIC: Controlled descent. Depth is valid when hand-to-torso 
  distance is < 25% of torso length.
* BOTTOM: Transition zone. 
* CONCENTRIC: Explosive push back to lockout (> 70% torso length).

3. KINETIC CHAIN PENALTIES
--------------------------
* HIP_SAG (-15): Hips dropping below the Shoulder-Ankle line. 
  Indicates core failure.
* HIP_PIKE (-10): Hips pushing upward to shift load to shoulders.
* ELBOW_FLARE (-10): Elbows exceeding 60° relative to the torso. 
  Triggers warning for shoulder health.
* HEAD_DROP (-5): "Nodding" or reaching with the face instead 
  of the chest.



4. TECHNICAL ADVANTAGE
----------------------
By using the Plank-Aligned Normalizer, "Hip Sag" is no longer 
a complex calculation; it is a simple Y-coordinate drift of 
the ankle landmark relative to the (0,0) hip origin.
