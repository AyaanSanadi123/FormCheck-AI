================================================================
VISUALIZER: HANGING STABILITY STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Plumb-Line Overlay)
================================================================

1. THE PLUMB LINE
-----------------
A thin vertical guide is projected from the Bar (Wrist) to 
the bottom of the frame. If the Hip drifts away from this 
line, it indicates a lack of core control (Swinging).

2. COMPRESSION TARGET
---------------------
A horizontal 'Ghost Line' extends from the Hip. The user's 
Ankle must reach or pass this line to trigger the 'TOP' 
state and ensure a full 90° range of motion.

3. KINETIC CHAIN COLORING
-------------------------
* Torso: Remains WHITE when stable.
* Legs: Turn GREEN when straight and engaged.
* Legs: Turn RED if micro-flexion (Knee Bend) is detected.



4. MOMENTUM WARNING
-------------------
The Plumb Line turns RED if the 'Swing Coefficient' is 
violated, providing instant feedback to stop and reset.
