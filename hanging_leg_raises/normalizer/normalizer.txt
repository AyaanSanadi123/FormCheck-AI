================================================================
NORMALIZER: BAR-ANCHORED CANONICAL POSE
Version: 1.0 (Suspension-Centric Transformation)
================================================================

1. THE "SUSPENSION ANCHOR"
--------------------------
Traditional normalizers anchor the hip or foot. For hanging 
exercises, the Wrist (Bar) is the only fixed point. This 
Normalizer maps the Wrist to (0,0), making all body movement 
relative to the point of suspension.

2. COORDINATE MAPPING
---------------------
* Wrist: (0, 0) - The Anchor Point.
* Shoulder: Directly below Wrist (~0, +Y).
* Hip: Follows the Shoulder vertically in a 'Dead Hang'.
* Legs: Move from (+Y) into (+X, +Y) space during the rep.

3. SWING DETECTION GEOMETRY
---------------------------
By normalizing the vertical axis, the 'Swing Coefficient' is 
calculated simply by checking the Hip's X-coordinate. 
- Hip X > 0.2: Torso is swinging forward.
- Hip X < -0.2: Torso is swinging backward (Kipping).

4. 90-DEGREE VERIFICATION
-------------------------
Scaling by torso length ensures that when the Ankle reaches 
the same Y-coordinate as the Hip, the legs are parallel 
to the floor (90° flexion).
