================================================================
PLANK REP LOGIC: ISOMETRIC INTEGRITY STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Stability Timer System)
================================================================

1. PRIMARY MECHANICAL GOAL
--------------------------
To maintain a rigid, linear bridge between the shoulders and 
ankles, resisting gravity's pull on the lumbar spine.

2. STATE MACHINE (Isometric Timer)
---------------------------
* IDLE: Baseline search.
* HOLDING: Accumulates 'Reps' (1 rep = 1 second of perfect form).
* FAILED: Timer pauses when hip deviation exceeds 15% of torso length.

3. INTEGRITY PENALTIES
--------------------------
* HIP_SAG (-10): Hips drop below the Shoulder-Ankle line. 
  Indicates core failure and potential lumbar strain.
* HIP_PIKE (-10): Hips rise above the line. Indicates the user 
  is shifting weight to the shoulders to avoid core work.
* NECK_DIVE (-5): Head dropping or looking up. 
* STABILITY_LOSS (-15): Detected via high-frequency jitter in 
  the Hip Y-axis. Indicates neural fatigue.



4. TECHNICAL ADVANTAGE
----------------------
By defining 'Reps' as seconds of quality, we gamify the 
isometric hold while ensuring the user doesn't 'cheat the clock' 
with bad form.
