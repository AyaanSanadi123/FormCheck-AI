================================================================
PLANK NORMALIZER: STRUCTURAL BEAM STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Elbow-Anchored Canonical Pose)
================================================================

1. COMPONENT PURPOSE
--------------------
Unlike dynamic exercises, the Plank Normalizer creates a 
mathematical "Straight Edge." By aligning the Shoulder and 
Ankle to Y=0, any deviation of the Spine or Hip is 
instantly quantifiable as a Y-axis error.

2. COORDINATE MAPPING
---------------------
* Elbow: Always (0, 0) - The foundation of the pose.
* Shoulder: Anchored near the origin on the horizontal plane.
* Ankle: Anchored far on the X-axis at Y=0.
* Hip: In a perfect plank, Y should be 0.0.
  - Negative Y: Piking (Hips too high).
  - Positive Y: Sagging (Hips too low).

3. WHY THIS IS "GOLD STANDARD"
------------------------------
A. Tilt Immunity: Camera tilt usually makes a plank look like 
   it is sagging or piking. This rotation cancels that out.
B. Depth Precision: Because we scale by the user's own torso 
   length, we can set universal thresholds for "Sag" (e.g., 
   a 10% deviation of torso length is a fail).
