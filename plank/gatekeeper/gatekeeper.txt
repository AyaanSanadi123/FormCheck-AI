1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Enforces a strict side-view to monitor the Ear-Shoulder-Hip-Ankle 
alignment and ensures joint stacking (Elbow under Shoulder).

2. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS (check)
----------------------------------
A. Joint Stacking:
- Monitors the X-offset between Shoulder and Elbow. A vertical 
  humerus is required to prevent rotator cuff strain.

B. The "Stability Lock":
- Requires 60 frames (2 seconds at 30fps) of near-perfect 
  immobility to establish the "Zero-Sag" baseline.

C. Side-Profile Confirmation:
- Filters for >90% visibility on the camera-facing side.

3. CALIBRATION OUTPUTS
----------------------
* active_side: LEFT or RIGHT side for tracking.
* scale_factor: Torso length (Shoulder-to-Hip) for normalizing 
  sag/pike measurements.
