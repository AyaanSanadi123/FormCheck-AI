================================================================
PLANK VISUALIZER: ISOMETRIC STABILITY STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Structural Integrity UI)
================================================================

1. THE "REFERENCE BEAM"
-----------------------
A grey guide line is drawn between the Shoulder and Ankle. 
This represents the 'Perfect Neutral' axis. The user's 
actual spinal lines (Sh-Hip-Ank) are projected against this.

2. SAG/PIKE DETECTION
---------------------
The Hip-to-Spine segments turn RED if the Hip Y-coordinate 
deviates more than 15% from the Reference Beam. 
* Above the line: PIKING.
* Below the line: SAGGING.

3. JOINT STACKING RAIL
----------------------
A vertical indicator connects the Shoulder to the Elbow. 
If this line tilts significantly, it turns ORANGE, signaling 
poor weight distribution on the joints.



4. STABILITY HUD
----------------
Replaces the 'Rep Counter' with a 'Time Counter'. Seconds 
only accumulate when the structural lines are GREEN.
