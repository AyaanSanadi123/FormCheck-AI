# SKULL CRUSHER REP LOGIC: DETAILED PHYSICS & SCORING REPORT
# Version: 1.0
# Blueprint Standard: Compliant

## 1. THE GEOMETRIC ENGINE: "THE SUPINE PIVOT"
The Skull Crusher is an isolation movement performed lying down. Because the Normalizer pinned the Shoulder to (0,0) and mathematically rotated the user so their head is in the -X quadrant and feet are in the +X quadrant, the upper arm should be completely rigid in the +Y space (pointing to the ceiling).
* **The Pivot (Elbow):** Must remain at its baseline (X, Y) coordinate above the chest.
* **The Lever (Forearm):** Moves in a downward arc toward the head.
* **The Metric:** We track the interior angle of `Shoulder -> Elbow -> Wrist`.
    * ~180°: Arm is straight (Lockout/Start).
    * 90° or less: Forearm is parallel to the floor (Max stretch).

## 2. THE REP CYCLE (ECCENTRIC-FIRST STATE MACHINE)
Because the user begins the lift at lockout and fights gravity downward, the standard state machine must be Eccentric-First.

### State 1: IDLE (Lockout)
* **Goal:** Arms extended upward, bracing for the rep.
* **Trigger to ECCENTRIC:** Angular velocity becomes significantly negative (angle is decreasing rapidly below 160°).

### State 2: ECCENTRIC (Lowering Phase)
* **Goal:** Lower the bar under control toward the forehead.
* **Peak Detection:** We track `min_drop_angle`. The moment angular velocity flips positive, the user is pushing the weight back up.

### State 3: CONCENTRIC (Pushing Phase)
* **Goal:** Contract the triceps to return to a 180° lockout.
* **Completion:** The angle returns above 160°.

## 3. FAULT DETECTION MATRIX & SCORING HIERARCHY
Following the blueprint, reps start at 100 points. We apply specific deductions based on mechanical severity.

### Severe Deductions (-10 Points)
**1. SHOULDER_SWING (The Lat Pullover Cheat)**
* **The Cheat:** Letting the elbow drift backward over the head during the eccentric, then using the lats to heave it forward during the concentric.
* **The Math:** Euclidean distance between the live `Elbow(x,y)` and the normalized `baseline_el(x,y)`. If this distance exceeds 0.20 "Arm Units", the mechanical anchor is broken.

### Minor Deductions (-5 Points)
**1. SHALLOW_DROP (Incomplete Eccentric)**
* **The Cheat:** Stopping the bar high above the forehead to avoid the hardest part of the ROM.
* **The Math:** Evaluated at the transition from ECCENTRIC to CONCENTRIC. If `min_drop_angle > 90.0°`, they shorted the stretch.

**2. SHORT_LOCKOUT (Incomplete Concentric)**
* **The Cheat:** Starting the next rep before fully locking out the triceps.
* **The Math:** If a new ECCENTRIC phase begins (velocity flips negative) before the `current_angle` recovers to at least 160°.
