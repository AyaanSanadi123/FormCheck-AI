# CABLE KICKBACK NORMALIZER: STRATEGY & MATH
# Version: 1.0
# Blueprint Standard: Compliant

## 1. THE CANONICAL POSE
Per the pipeline blueprint, the Normalizer translates the raw pixels into a universal geometric standard. For the Cable Kickback, the primary mechanical pivot is the shoulder joint.

Our Canonical Pose is defined as:
* **The Pivot (Origin):** The active **Shoulder** is mathematically locked at `(0, 0)`.
* **The Y-Axis (Vertical):** Inverted so that UP towards the ceiling is positive (`+Y`).
* **The X-Axis (Horizontal):** Normalized so the user is always facing RIGHT (`+X`). Because they are pushing the cable backward, the arm will extend into the negative space (`-X`).
* **The Scale:** All coordinates are divided by the `scale_factor` (Upper Arm Length) captured by the Gatekeeper.

## 2. THE THREAD-SAFE INJECTION
We utilize the explicitly passed argument structure for `_normalize_point` to ensure that if the Visualizer calls the Normalizer before the Gatekeeper finishes its 1.2-second calibration, the Normalizer's internal baseline variables are not permanently corrupted.

## 3. IMPACT ON REP LOGIC
By enforcing this Canonical Pose, the Rep Logic can blindly assume:
1. **The Anchor:** The Elbow should hover near `(-1.0, Y)` depending on how high they rowed it. 
2. **The "Pendulum Swing" Cheat:** If the Elbow's X-coordinate suddenly spikes toward `0` (the shoulder) or goes positive, we immediately know they dropped their elbow to swing the weight!
3. **The Kickback:** A perfect rep will see the Wrist travel from a flexed position near the shoulder out to roughly `(-2.0, Y)` as the arm locks out backward.
