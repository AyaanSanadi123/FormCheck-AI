# CABLE TRICEP KICKBACK GATEKEEPER: STRATEGY & LOGIC
# Version: 1.0
# Blueprint Standard: Compliant

## 1. THE VIEWPOINT & OCCLUSION
The Cable Kickback requires a sagittal (side-profile) view of the working arm. 
* **Blueprint Compliance:** The Gatekeeper isolates the `active_side` based on the highest visibility score, immediately discarding the occluded side of the body.

## 2. THE SETUP HEURISTICS (GEOMETRY CHECK)
To begin tracking, the user must be bent over, facing the cable stack, with their elbow rowed up to their ribcage and their arm bent, ready to kick back.

* **The Hinge Check (Torso Angle):** The `Shoulder -> Hip` vector must be between 0° (parallel to the floor) and 65° (bent over). If they stand fully upright, it becomes a pushdown.
* **The "Pinned Elbow" Check:** The upper arm must be rowed back. The Elbow's Y-coordinate must be relatively high (close to the Shoulder's Y-coordinate). If the elbow is hanging straight down, they haven't set their anchor.
* **The Flexion Check (Starting ROM):** The user must start at the bottom of the rep. The `Shoulder -> Elbow -> Wrist` angle must be less than 110°.

## 3. STABILITY & CALIBRATION
* **Stability:** Uses the 2D variance matrix (checking X and Y for both the wrist and elbow) to ensure the user has stabilized the cable tension before starting the rep.
* **Calibration Data (The Passport):** * *Scale Factor:* Because this is a pure arm isolation, we will set the `scale_factor` to the **Upper Arm Length** (Shoulder to Elbow distance) instead of the Torso Length.
    * *Origin:* We capture `shoulder_origin_x` and `y`.
    * *Anchors:* We capture `baseline_el_x` and `baseline_el_y` to strictly police the "Pendulum Swing" fault in the Rep Logic.
    * *Setup Angle:* We capture `setup_torso_angle` to police "Torso Bobbing".
