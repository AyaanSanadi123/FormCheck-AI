================================================================
PREACHER CURL REP LOGIC: STANDARDS & BIOMECHANICS
Version: 1.0 (Strict Isolation Focus)
================================================================

1. PRIMARY METRIC
-----------------
* Elbow Flexion Angle: Measures the angle between Shoulder-Elbow-Wrist.
* Shoulder Stability: Measures vertical Y-displacement of the shoulder 
  to ensure the upper arm remains fixed against the bench pad.

2. STATE MACHINE (Up-First)
---------------------------
1. IDLE: Arm extended on the pad (Angle > 160°).
2. CONCENTRIC: Forearm moving toward the shoulder.
3. TOP: Peak contraction reached (Angle < 45°).
4. ECCENTRIC: Controlled lowering toward the bottom of the bench.
5. COMPLETE: Transitions back to IDLE once full extension is met.

3. FAULT DETECTION
------------------
* ELBOW_LIFT (-15): Triggered if the shoulder migrates vertically. 
  Indicates the user is "peeling" their arm off the pad to use 
  momentum or front delts.
* SHORT_ROM (-20): Triggered if the user fails to reach at least 
  160° extension. This is critical as the preacher curl provides 
  maximum tension at the bottom.



4. TECHNICAL NOTES
------------------
By anchoring the Elbow at (0,0) in the Normalizer, the shoulder 
should theoretically reside at (0, -1.0) in normalized units. 
Any drift from this Y-coordinate indicates poor pad contact.
