================================================================
PREACHER CURL GATEKEEPER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
Version: 1.0 (Fixed-Pivot Calibration)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Ensures the user is positioned sideways with their upper arm 
supported by a preacher bench. Establishes the Humerus (Upper Arm) 
as the scale factor for movement normalization.

2. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS (check)
----------------------------------
A. Visibility:
- Primary focus: Shoulder (11/12), Elbow (13/14), and Wrist (15/16).
- High visibility (>0.85) ensures the arm is not hidden behind 
  the bench structure.

B. Orientation:
- Z-Depth Variance: Confirms a sagittal view to accurately measure 
  the pivot angle of the elbow.

C. Initial State (Full Extension):
- Requires an initial elbow angle > 150°. This ensures the user 
  starts from the most vulnerable/important part of the ROM.

3. CALIBRATION OUTPUTS
----------------------
* scale_factor: Humerus length. Used to normalize the forearm 
  swing radius.
* elbow_y_anchor: The static vertical position of the elbow. 
  Essential for detecting "Elbow Lift" (cheating) during the rep.
