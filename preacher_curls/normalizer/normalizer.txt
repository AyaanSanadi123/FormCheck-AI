================================================================
PREACHER CURL NORMALIZER: STANDARDIZATION LOGIC
Version: 1.0 (Elbow-Origin Canonical Pose)
================================================================

1. COMPONENT PURPOSE
------------------------------
Transforms raw pose data into a consistent mathematical space 
where the preacher pad's pivot (the Elbow) is the center (0,0). 
This removes camera distance and user height as variables.

2. STANDARDIZATION RULES
----------------------------------
A. Origin (Translation):
- The Elbow (ID 13/14) is set to (0,0). Since the arm is fixed 
  against the pad, this provides a stable anchor for measuring 
  forearm rotation and shoulder drift.

B. Facing (Reflection):
- X-axis is reflected if the user is facing LEFT.
- RESULT: The forearm always curls toward the POSITIVE X direction.

C. Scale (Normalization):
- All spatial data is divided by the 'scale_factor' (Humerus Length).
- RESULT: A forearm length will be approximately 1.0 to 1.2 units, 
  making angular thresholds highly predictable.



3. IMPACT ON REP LOGIC
-------------------------------
By anchoring the Elbow at (0,0), any movement of the Shoulder 
in the Y-axis immediately flags an "Elbow Lift" fault, as the 
humerus length is the expected radius of the pivot.
