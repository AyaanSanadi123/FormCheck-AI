================================================================
PREACHER CURL VISUALIZER: RENDERING STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Isolation Tracking UI)
================================================================

1. VISUAL FOCUS
---------------
The visualizer emphasizes the 'Pad Contact Zone'. The segment 
from the Shoulder to the Elbow turns RED if the 'ELBOW_LIFT' 
fault is detected, indicating the humerus is no longer flush 
with the preacher bench.

2. HUD ELEMENTS
---------------
* Header: Shows Rep count, current Elbow Flexion Angle, and Score.
* Angle Tracker: Displays real-time degrees to help the user 
  visualize the ROM requirement (e.g., reaching > 160°).



3. FEEDBACK TOASTS
------------------
* "KEEP ARMS FLUSH": Warning for elbow lift.
* "EXTEND FULLY": Encouragement to hit the bottom range.
* "SQUEEZE AND HOLD": Triggered at the TOP state.
