================================================================
CALF RAISE NORMALIZER: STANDARDIZATION STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Toe-Origin Canonical Pose)
================================================================

1. COMPONENT PURPOSE
------------------------------
Standardizes raw MediaPipe landmarks so that the Calf Raise 
analysis is invariant to user height, camera distance, or 
facing direction.

2. STANDARDIZATION RULES
----------------------------------
A. Origin (Translation):
- The Toe (ID 31/32) is set as (0,0). This ensures that 
  the 'floor' is consistently anchored at Y=0 relative to the 
  pivot of the foot.

B. Facing (Reflection):
- X-axis reflection is applied if the Gatekeeper detects the 
  user is facing LEFT.
- RESULT: The heel always resides at a NEGATIVE X-coordinate 
  relative to the toe.

C. Scale (Normalization):
- All spatial data is divided by the 'scale_factor' (Torso Length).
- RESULT: A heel rise of 0.2 units represents a consistent 
  percentage of body size regardless of pixel resolution.



3. IMPACT ON REP LOGIC
-------------------------------
By fixing the toe at the origin, the Rep Logic can verify 
form using simple absolute thresholds (e.g., "Heel Y < -0.15") 
rather than complex relative calculations.
