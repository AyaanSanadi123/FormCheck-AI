================================================================
CALF RAISE VISUALIZER: RENDERING STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Plantarflexion Overlay)
================================================================

1. COMPONENT PURPOSE
--------------------
Accepts Rep Logic packets to provide visual reinforcement of 
heel elevation and knee stability.

2. VISUAL ELEMENTS
------------------
* Primary Lever: Connects Knee to Ankle to Heel. The segment 
  between the Knee and Ankle turns RED if 'KNEE_BEND' is detected 
  during the concentric phase.
* Ground Anchor: A reference line drawn at the Toe (origin) 
  to visualize the vertical gap created by the heel lift.

3. COLOR & HUD STANDARDS
------------------------
* Follows Blueprint: Green (Good), Orange (Warning), Red (Fail).
* HUD: Top-aligned black bar showing Reps, State, and Score.
* Toast: Centered bottom feedback (e.g., "HOLD AT THE TOP").



4. ROBUSTNESS
-------------
* If the user moves out of frame (packet=None), the visualizer 
  renders a "SEARCHING..." warning instead of crashing.
