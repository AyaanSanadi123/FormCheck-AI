================================================================
CALF RAISE GATEKEEPER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
Version: 1.0 (Sagittal Foot Alignment)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Ensures the user is positioned in a side-profile view to accurately 
measure the vertical displacement of the calcaneus (heel) relative 
to the metatarsals (toes).

2. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS (check)
----------------------------------
A. Visibility:
- Focuses on the lower kinetic chain (24-32). 
- All critical joints must exceed 0.85 visibility.

B. Profile Depth:
- Uses shoulder Z-depth variance (> 0.20) to ensure the user is not 
  facing the camera directly.

C. Starting Geometry:
- Requires "Feet Flat." Calibration resets if the user begins 
  mid-rep or with heels already elevated.

3. CALIBRATION OUTPUTS
----------------------
* active_side: "LEFT" or "RIGHT" based on joint visibility.
* scale_factor: Torso length used to normalize heel elevation.
* floor_y: Established Y-coordinate for the ground plane.
* heel_y_baseline: Baseline Y for the heel at rest.
