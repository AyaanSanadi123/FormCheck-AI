================================================================
LUNGE GATEKEEPER: UNILATERAL STABILITY STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Side-Profile Calibration)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Establishes the 'Lead Leg' for unilateral tracking and captures 
the floor baseline to measure back-knee depth accurately.

2. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS (check)
----------------------------------
A. Side-Profile Confirmation:
- Requires high visibility (>85%) of the shoulder-to-toe 
  chain on the active side.

B. Stability Window:
- 45 frames of static upright posture to define the 
  'Vertical Axis' of the starting position.

C. Floor Indexing:
- Sets Y=0 at the front ankle to calculate the back knee's 
  distance from the ground during the eccentric phase.

3. CALIBRATION OUTPUTS
----------------------
* active_side: Determines which leg is the 'Front' leg.
* scale_factor: Torso length used for depth normalization.
* floor_y_baseline: Absolute Y-coordinate of the floor.
