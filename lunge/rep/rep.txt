LUNGE REP LOGIC: UNILATERAL PERFORMANCE STANDARDS
Version: 1.1 (Sagittal Plane Integrity)
================================================================

1. PRIMARY MECHANICAL GOAL
--------------------------
Achieve 90/90 knee flexion while maintaining a vertical torso 
and keeping the front knee stacked over the front ankle.

2. STATE MACHINE (Down-First)
---------------------------
* IDLE: Standing upright, legs staggered.
* ECCENTRIC: Controlled descent. Back knee must reach Y > -0.15 
  (relative to floor Y=0).
* BOTTOM: Transition point where downward velocity stops.
* CONCENTRIC: Explosive drive through the front heel.
* COMPLETE: Return to initial standing height.

3. KINETIC CHAIN PENALTIES
--------------------------
* KNEE_SHEAR (-20): Front knee travels too far forward (X > 0.25). 
  Places excessive load on the patellar tendon.
* HIP_DRIFT (-15): Horizontal swaying. Indicates lack of 
  core and gluteal stability.
* TORSO_LEAN (-10): Excessive forward lean. Shifts load from 
  the glutes to the lower back.
* KNEE_WOBBLE (-15): Lateral instability (Z-axis deviation).



4. TECHNICAL ADVANTAGE
----------------------
Using the Front Ankle as (0,0) makes "Knee Over Ankle" 
monitoring a zero-cost comparison of the knee's X-coordinate.
