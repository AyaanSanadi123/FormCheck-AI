LUNGE NORMALIZER: FLOOR-CENTRIC STANDARDS
Version: 1.1 (Unilateral-Horizontal Canonical Pose)
================================================================

1. THE "STABLE FLOOR" TRANSFORMATION
-----------------------------------------
This module uses the Front Ankle and Toe as a level-guide. 
Even if the camera is tilted, the normalizer forces the 
floor to be the Y=0 plane.

2. COORDINATE MAPPING
---------------------
* Front Ankle: (0, 0) - The primary pivot point.
* Front Toe: (+X, 0) - Defines the floor direction.
* Hips: Move along a vertical arc (-Y space).
* Back Knee: Approaches (X_dist, 0) during the bottom of the rep.

3. BIOMECHANICAL INSIGHTS
-------------------------
* Vertical Shin: If the Front Knee's X-coordinate is 0, 
  the shin is perfectly vertical.
* Hip Drift: Any X-axis deviation of the Hip relative to its 
  starting X-position indicates horizontal instability.
* 90/90 Check: Scaling by torso length allows us to verify 
  that knee flexion matches the user's limb proportions.
