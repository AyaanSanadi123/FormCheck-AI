================================================================
LUNGE VISUALIZER: SAGITTAL STABILITY STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Unilateral Feedback UI)
================================================================

1. VERTICAL SHIN RAIL
---------------------
A fixed guide line projects upward from the Front Ankle. 
The front leg (Ankle-Knee) turns RED if the knee drifts past 
this rail, indicating excessive shear stress.

2. TORSO VECTOR
---------------
Highlights the alignment between the Hip and Shoulder. 
Turns RED if the angle exceeds 25° from vertical, indicating 
a collapsed chest or core failure.

3. DEPTH INDICATOR
------------------
Monitors the trailing knee. The joint marker turns GREEN 
when the knee enters the "Depth Zone" (near the floor plane).



4. STABILITY OVERLAYS
---------------------
Horizontal arrows appear at the Hip level if 'Hip Drift' 
is detected, signaling a lack of sagittal control.
