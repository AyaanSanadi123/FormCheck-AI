Version: 1.1 (Scapular-Sweep UI)

1. THE "T-SPINE" CROSSHAIR
--------------------------
Renders a vertical axis at the midpoint of the shoulders. 
This serves as the 'Zero' anchor for measuring the symmetrical 
expansion of the rear deltoids.

2. ARM PATH MONITORING
----------------------
* Elbow Isometry: Arm segments turn RED if the elbow angle 
  changes significantly from the starting position, indicating 
  'Rowing' instead of 'Sweeping'.
* Scapular Box: The line between shoulders turns RED if the 
  user shrugs (vertical Y-drift).



3. SCORE & STATE HUD
--------------------
* Green: High-quality reps with minimal shrugging.
* Red: Active faults detected (e.g., ELBOW_PIVOT).
* Orange: State transitions (e.g., CONCENTRIC).
