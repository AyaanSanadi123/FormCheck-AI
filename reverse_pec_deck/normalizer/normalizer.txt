================================================================
REVERSE PEC DECK NORMALIZER: POSTERIOR STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (T-Spine Canonical Pose)
================================================================

1. COMPONENT PURPOSE
------------------------------
Standardizes the frame so the mid-back (T-Spine) is at (0,0). 
This allows the Rep Logic to track the 'Sweep' of the rear 
deltoids as a purely horizontal (X-axis) expansion.

2. STANDARDIZATION RULES
----------------------------------
A. Origin (Translation):
- Midpoint of landmarks 11 and 12 is set to (0,0). 
- RESULT: Hand positions are now relative to the spinal center.

B. Rotation (Vertical Alignment):
- Rotates the entire coordinate system so the vector from 
  the Mid-Shoulder to Mid-Hip is parallel to the Y-axis.
- EFFECT: Neutralizes camera tilt and user leaning.

C. Scale (Normalization):
- All spatial data is divided by 'scale_factor' (Shoulder Width).
- RESULT: A wrist 'X' value of 1.2 means the hand has traveled 
  1.2 shoulder-widths away from the spine.



3. IMPACT ON REP LOGIC
-------------------------------
By fixing the spine at X=0, we can detect 'Asymmetrical Pulling' 
if the absolute X-value of the Left Wrist differs from the 
Right Wrist. We can also detect 'Shrugging' by monitoring the 
Y-drift of the shoulders away from the 0.0 baseline.
