================================================================
REVERSE PEC DECK REP LOGIC: ISOLATION STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Rear Delt Sweep Focus)
================================================================

1. PRIMARY MECHANICAL GOAL
--------------------------
Horizontal abduction of the humerus. The wrists should move 
outward in an arc centered on the T-Spine (0,0).

2. STATE MACHINE (Concentric-First)
---------------------------
* IDLE: Arms in front, holding handles.
* CONCENTRIC: Sweeping outward. Target expansion is > 1.1 
  normalized shoulder widths.
* TOP: Maximum abduction reached. 
* ECCENTRIC: Slow, controlled return to the midline.

3. ISOLATION PENALTIES
--------------------------
* ELBOW_PIVOT (-20): Triggered if the elbow angle changes by 
  more than 15°. Indicates the user is 'rowing' or using 
  triceps extension rather than rear delt sweep.
* SHOULDER_SHRUG (-15): Shoulders drifting up (Negative Y). 
  Indicates upper trap dominance over posterior delts.
* ASYMMETRY (-10): Left and right expansion variance > 15%.



4. TECHNICAL ADVANTAGE
----------------------
By tracking 'Elbow Isometry' against an initial baseline 
captured at the start of the rep, we ensure the movement 
remains a fly and not a press/row variation.
