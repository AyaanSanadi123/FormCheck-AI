# DIPS VISUALIZER: STRATEGY & UX
# Version: 1.0
# Blueprint Standard: Compliant

## 1. THE CHALLENGE: "VISUALIZING DEPTH AND SWING"
Dips are strictly evaluated by two things: absolute depth (shoulder crossing the elbow) and absolute core stability (no swinging). The UI must instantly highlight when these two mechanics break down.

## 2. THE VISUAL SOLUTIONS

### A. The "Dynamic Depth Gauge"
* **Concept:** A horizontal line extending out from the user's elbow. This is the "finish line."
* **Visual:** * White/Blue = Dropping down.
    * Flashing Green = The shoulder physically crosses below the line (Perfect Depth!).
    * Flashing Yellow = "SHALLOW_REP" fault. The user reversed the rep before the shoulder crossed the line.

### B. The "Pendulum Alert" (Torso Line)
* **Concept:** We draw a thick line connecting the active Hip to the active Shoulder.
* **Visual:**
    * Green/White = Core is tight, body is dropping straight down.
    * Flashing Red = "PENDULUM_SWING" fault. The torso line turns red and thickens to indicate the hips are swinging horizontally out of the vertical drop channel.

### C. The Arm Lever & HUD
* **Concept:** Render the arm (Shoulder -> Elbow -> Wrist).
* **Visual:**
    * White = Good form.
    * Yellow = "SHORT_LOCKOUT" triggered, indicating the user didn't fully extend their arms at the top.
* Standardized HUD and toast messages per the PIPELINE_BLUEPRINT.md.
