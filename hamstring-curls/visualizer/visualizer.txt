# HAMSTRING CURL VISUALIZER: STRATEGY & UX
# Version: 1.0

## 1. THE CHALLENGE: "ISOLATION FEEDBACK"
In a lying hamstring curl, the user is prone and cannot see the machine or their legs. The visualizer must provide high-contrast feedback that emphasizes the two critical zones: the hip (stability) and the ankle (range of motion).

## 2. THE VISUAL SOLUTIONS

### A. The "Radial Protractor"
* **Concept:** Since the knee is the origin (0,0), we draw a semi-transparent radial gauge. 
* **Visual:** A circular arc trailing the ankle.
    * White/Cyan = Active movement.
    * Red = Top of the rep if "SHORT_CURL" is triggered.
* **Why:** Clearly visualizes the 0° to 90°+ arc of the rep.

### B. The "Hip Stability Bar"
* **Concept:** A horizontal line drawn through the hip's starting baseline position.
* **Visual:**
    * Green = Hip is flush with the pad.
    * Red = "HIP_LIFT" fault (The hip landmark has risen too far above the line).
* **Why:** Alerts the user to the most common cheating mechanism (using the lower back).

### C. Rep Progress HUD
* **Concept:** A top-bar display for rep count and current form score.
