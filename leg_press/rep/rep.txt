================================================================
LEG PRESS REP LOGIC: STANDARDS & SAFETY
Version: 1.0 (Joint Protection focus)
================================================================

1. CORE METRICS
---------------
* Knee Flexion Angle: Monitors depth (Bottom) and dangerous lockouts (Top).
* Pelvic Displacement (Butt-Wink): Tracks if the Hip (Origin) migrates 
  off the baseline seat Y-coordinate.
* Knee Tracking: Monitors lateral (X-axis) deviation of the knee 
  relative to the ankle.

2. STATE MACHINE (Down-First)
-----------------------------
1. IDLE: Legs extended, sled stationary.
2. ECCENTRIC: Controlled lowering phase. Minimum depth requirement: 0.65 units.
3. BOTTOM: Momentary transition point.
4. CONCENTRIC: Power phase pushing the sled back.
5. COMPLETE: Finalizes scoring and increments rep count.

3. FAULT DETECTION
------------------
* KNEE_LOCKOUT (-25): Triggered if knee angle > 175°. High risk for 
  joint injury under heavy load.
* BUTT_WINK (-15): Pelvis lifting off the seat. Causes lumbar 
  rounding and spinal risk.
* KNEE_VALGUS (-10): Knees caving inward during the push.



4. TECHNICAL NOTES
------------------
Uses scale_factor (Torso) to ensure a "deep" rep (0.65) is 
proportionally consistent regardless of the user's height.
