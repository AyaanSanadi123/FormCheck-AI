================================================================
LEG PRESS GATEKEEPER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
Version: 1.0 (Sagittal Profile Calibration)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Verifies that the user is seated in a leg press machine with a 
clear side-profile view. Establishes the torso scale to measure 
relative sled depth and hip migration.

2. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS (check)
----------------------------------
A. Visibility:
- Primary focus: Hip (24), Knee (26), Ankle (28).
- High confidence (>0.85) required to ensure machine handles or 
  padding are not obscuring the joint pivots.

B. Orientation:
- Sagittal Plane Check: Ensures the user isn't facing the camera 
  directly (Z-depth variance check).

C. Initial State:
- Start Extension: Legs must be > 140° (Extended) to verify 
  calibration occurs at the lockout/rest position rather than 
  mid-compression.

3. CALIBRATION OUTPUTS
----------------------
* scale_factor: Torso length (Shoulder-Hip). Normalizes sled 
  depth across different machine sizes.
* hip_y_anchor: Critical for detecting "Butt-Wink" (Hip migration) 
  during the bottom of the rep.
