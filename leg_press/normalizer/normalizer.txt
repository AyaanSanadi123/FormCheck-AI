================================================================
LEG PRESS NORMALIZER: STANDARDIZATION LOGIC
Version: 1.0 (Hip-Origin Canonical Pose)
================================================================

1. COMPONENT PURPOSE
------------------------------
Standardizes the coordinate system so that the machine seat (Hip) 
is the center of the world. This allows the Rep Logic to 
measure sled "Depth" as a simple X/Y displacement of the ankle 
relative to the seat.

2. STANDARDIZATION RULES
----------------------------------
A. Origin (Translation):
- The Hip (ID 23/24) is moved to (0,0). Since the hip is 
  pressed against the seat, this creates a static reference 
  point for the entire kinetic chain.

B. Facing (Reflection):
- If the Gatekeeper detects a LEFT side profile, X is inverted.
- RESULT: The legs always extend toward the POSITIVE X-axis.

C. Scale (Normalization):
- All spatial data is divided by the 'scale_factor' (Torso Length).
- RESULT: A displacement of 1.0 unit corresponds to one torso 
  length of movement, making depth thresholds universal.



3. IMPACT ON REP LOGIC
-------------------------------
By normalizing to the Hip, 'Butt-Wink' or Hip-Lift detection 
becomes straightforward: if the normalized Hip-Y deviates 
significantly from 0.0, the user is losing contact with the 
seat padding.
