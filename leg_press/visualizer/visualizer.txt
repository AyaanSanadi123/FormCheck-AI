LEG PRESS VISUALIZER: RENDERING STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Safety-First UI)
================================================================

1. VISUAL PRIORITY
------------------
The visualizer focuses on the 'Safety Pivots':
* Hip Pivot: Highlights pelvic stability. Turns RED if the user's 
  lower back rounds or lifts off the seat.
* Knee Pivot: Highlights joint safety. Pulses RED if the user 
  locks their knees at the top of the movement.

2. HUD ELEMENTS
---------------
* Header: Standardized display for Reps, State, and Quality Score.
* Depth Tracker: A vertical gauge on the right side of the screen 
  illustrating the ankle's Y-position relative to the Hip-origin.



3. FEEDBACK TOASTS
------------------
* "AVOID LOCKING KNEES": High-priority warning triggered by 
  dangerous extension angles.
* "KEEP BACK FLAT": Triggered by hip migration/butt-wink.
* "PUSH!": Encouragement during the bottom transition.
