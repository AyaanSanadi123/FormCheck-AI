================================================================
TRICEP EXTENSION VISUALIZER: RENDERING STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Isolation Tracking UI)
================================================================

1. VISUAL PRIORITY
------------------
The visualizer focuses on the 'Shoulder-Elbow Axis'. If the shoulder 
migrates from its vertical anchor, the 'Pivot Lock' circle at the 
elbow turns RED, indicating the movement is no longer isolated.

2. HUD ELEMENTS
---------------
* Header: Real-time Rep count and Extension Angle.
* Extension Angle: Specifically tracks the 'Lockout' angle to 
  encourage the user to reach >160° at the bottom.



3. FEEDBACK TOASTS
------------------
* "PIN ELBOWS AT SIDES": High-priority warning for shoulder drive.
* "SQUEEZE AT THE BOTTOM": Triggered during the peak lockout.
* "CONTROL THE RISE": Triggered during the eccentric phase.
