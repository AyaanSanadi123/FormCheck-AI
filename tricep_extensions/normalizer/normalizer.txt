================================================================
TRICEP EXTENSION NORMALIZER: STANDARDIZATION LOGIC
Version: 1.0 (Elbow-Origin Canonical Pose)
================================================================

1. COMPONENT PURPOSE
------------------------------
Standardizes the coordinate system so that the primary pivot 
point (the Elbow) is the center of the world (0,0). This enables 
the detection of minute form errors that would be lost in 
unnormalized pixel space.

2. STANDARDIZATION RULES
----------------------------------
A. Origin (Translation):
- The Elbow (ID 13/14) is set to (0,0). In a perfect rep, the 
  Shoulder should reside at roughly (0, -1.0) and the Wrist 
  should rotate in an arc around the origin.

B. Facing (Reflection):
- X-axis is reflected if the user is facing LEFT.
- RESULT: The "Pushdown" or "Extension" always travels into the 
  POSITIVE X region of the coordinate plane.

C. Scale (Normalization):
- All spatial data is divided by the 'scale_factor' (Humerus Length).
- RESULT: Movement is measured in "Arm Lengths" rather than pixels, 
  making the logic invariant to camera distance.



3. IMPACT ON REP LOGIC
-------------------------------
By anchoring the Elbow at (0,0), "Shoulder Drive" is identified 
if the Shoulder-Y moves significantly from -1.0. "Partial Reps" 
are identified by checking if the Wrist-X reaches its maximum 
theoretical distance from the origin.
