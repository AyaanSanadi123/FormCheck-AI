TRICEP EXTENSION REP LOGIC: STANDARDS & BIOMECHANICS
Version: 1.0 (Strict Isolation Focus)
================================================================

1. PRIMARY METRIC
-----------------
* Elbow Extension Angle: Monitors the transition from stretch (<60°) 
  to full lockout (>160°).
* Shoulder-Elbow Fixation: Monitors the stability of the Humerus. 
  The Elbow must remain the only moving pivot.

2. STATE MACHINE (Down-First)
-----------------------------
1. IDLE: Bar held at chest height, triceps stretched.
2. CONCENTRIC: The "Push" phase where the arm straightens.
3. BOTTOM: Peak contraction/lockout point.
4. ECCENTRIC: The "Return" phase where the bar rises.
5. COMPLETE: Resets once the arm returns to the deep stretch position.

3. FAULT DETECTION
------------------
* SHOULDER_DRIVE (-15): Detected when the shoulder-y coordinate 
  shifts. Indicates the user is using their weight or chest to 
  press the bar down.
* SHORT_ROM (-20): Triggered if the user fails to fully stretch 
  the triceps (top) or fully lock them out (bottom).



4. TECHNICAL NOTES
------------------
Because the Normalizer anchors the Elbow at (0,0), any movement of 
the shoulder is measured as a deviation from the calibrated -1.0 
vertical position.
