================================================================
TRICEP EXTENSION GATEKEEPER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
Version: 1.0 (Sagittal Profile Calibration)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Ensures the user is positioned sideways for accurate sagittal 
analysis. Establishes the Humerus length as the scale factor 
to normalize the elbow's pivot radius.

2. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS (check)
----------------------------------
A. Visibility:
- Primary focus: Shoulder (11/12), Elbow (13/14), and Wrist (15/16).
- The hip (23/24) must also be visible to monitor torso lean.

B. Orientation:
- Z-Depth Variance: Confirms a side view. Frontal views are 
  rejected as they obscure the forearm-to-humerus angle.

C. Posture (Leaning):
- Detects if the user is leaning their body weight into the 
  bar (dx/dy angle from vertical). This ensures calibration 
  happens in a neutral, strict form position.

3. CALIBRATION OUTPUTS
----------------------
* scale_factor: Humerus length. Used to normalize the wrist's 
  swing radius.
* shoulder_x_anchor: Establishes the static X-plane. Any 
  significant drift in the elbow's X-coordinate relative to 
  this anchor during a rep indicates "Shoulder Drive" (cheating).
