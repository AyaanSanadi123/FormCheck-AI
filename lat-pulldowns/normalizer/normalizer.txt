# LAT PULLDOWN NORMALIZER: STRATEGY & MATH
# Version: 2.0 (With Yaw Correction)

## 1. THE PROBLEM: OBLIQUE ANGLES (+/- 45 deg)
* **Issue:** When viewing from an angle, the "far" side of the body appears compressed.
* **Result:** A perfectly symmetric pull looks asymmetric in 2D pixels. One elbow path looks vertical, the other looks diagonal.

## 2. THE SOLUTION: "VIRTUAL ROTATION"
We use the 3D depth (Z) from MediaPipe to mathematically rotate the user until they are facing the camera perfectly.

### Step A: Detect Yaw (Rotation Angle)
* **Metric:** The slope of the line connecting Left Shoulder to Right Shoulder in the X-Z plane.
* **Math:** `angle = arctan2(Left_Sh.z - Right_Sh.z, Left_Sh.x - Right_Sh.x)`.

### Step B: Rotate to "Flat"
* **Action:** Rotate all landmarks around the Spine Center (Y-axis) by `-angle`.
* **Result:** Left Shoulder Z = Right Shoulder Z. The user is now "virtually" facing strictly away from the camera.

### Step C: Center & Scale (Standardization)
* Same as before: Center X on Spine, Scale by Shoulder Width.

## 3. OUTPUT
* A standardized "Paper Doll" skeleton that looks like a perfect 2D diagram, regardless of whether the camera was at 0°, 30°, or 45°.
