================================================================
PULL-UP VISUALIZER: RENDERING STANDARDS
Version: 1.1 (Vertical Performance UI)
================================================================

1. THE "BAR PLANE" OVERLAY
--------------------------
Renders a white horizontal line representing the bar. 
The Chin indicator (at the mouth) turns from WHITE to GREEN 
once it crosses above this line, signaling a successful rep.

2. STABILITY MONITORING
-----------------------
* Kip Zone: Gray vertical boundaries illustrate the 'Safe Zone' 
  for horizontal movement. If the hips/torso drift outside 
  these lines, the torso skeleton turns RED.
* Scapular Depression: The neck lines turn RED if the user 
  pulls with 'shrugged' shoulders (Ear-Shoulder distance < THRESH).



3. FEEDBACK SYSTEM
------------------
* Toasts provide immediate correction: "STOP SWINGING", 
  "CHIN OVER BAR", or "DEPRESS SHOULDERS".
* The HUD keeps track of the 'State' (e.g., CONCENTRIC vs 
  TOP) to help users understand their current rep phase.
