PULL-UP NORMALIZER: REFINED STANDARDS
Version: 1.1 (Pendulum-Compensated Canonical Pose)
================================================================

1. THE "TRUE VERTICAL" TRANSFORMATION
-----------------------------------------
This version uses the Hip-to-Bar X-offset to calculate the 
hypotenuse of the swing. It corrects the Y-coordinate so 
the 'Pull' is measured only as the distance to the bar.

2. IMPACT ON ANALYSIS
---------------------
* Pivot Lock: The Bar is the absolute (0,0).
* Kipling Detection: Swing is separated from climb, allowing 
  the RepLogic to penalize 'Kicking' without losing track 
  of the rep.

3. COORDINATE MAPPING
---------------------
* Bar Midpoint: (0, 0)
* Chin (Top): Must reach < 0.0 (Negative Y)
* Elbows (Hang): Reach max Positive Y at the bottom.
