================================================================
PULL-UP GATEKEEPER: VERTICAL CALIBRATION STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Suspension Monitoring)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Verifies the 'Dead Hang' starting position and calibrates the 
horizontal plane of the bar based on wrist positioning.

2. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS (check)
----------------------------------
A. Suspension Detection:
- Confirms wrists (15, 16) are the highest points in the 
  kinetic chain.

B. Stability Window:
- Requires 45 frames of minimal movement to establish a 
  clean Torso Length scale factor.

C. Active Side:
- Set to 'BOTH' as pull-ups require bilateral symmetry 
  analysis.

3. CALIBRATION OUTPUTS
----------------------
* scale_factor: Average Torso Length (Shoulder to Hip).
* bar_y_baseline: The Y-coordinate of the bar, used to 
  determine rep completion (Chin-over-bar).
