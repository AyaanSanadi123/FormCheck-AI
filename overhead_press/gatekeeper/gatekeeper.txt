# OVERHEAD PRESS GATEKEEPER: OBJECTIVES & LOGIC
# Version: 1.0

## 1. THE PROBLEM: OCCLUSION & 3D RECONSTRUCTION
The Overhead Press, like other lifts, requires a clear view for accurate 3D analysis. The key challenges are:
1.  **Arm Occlusion:** If the camera is directly in front or behind the lifter, one arm will obscure the other, making it impossible to judge depth and lockout.
2.  **Angle for Normalizer:** The Normalizer relies on a side-view to rotate the skeleton into a perfect 2D profile for analysis. A front/back view provides no depth information to correct.

## 2. THE SOLUTION: "SIDE-VIEW ONLY" ENFORCEMENT
The Gatekeeper's primary job is to ensure the camera is positioned to the lifter's side.

### Validation Criteria:
* **Visibility Threshold:** All critical joints for the lift must be visible to the camera. This includes:
    * Wrists, Elbows, Shoulders (the pressing chain)
    * Hips (to monitor for excessive leaning)
* **Facing Angle:** Enforces a strict angle range (e.g., 60° to 120°) to guarantee a side-view. This allows the normalizer to function correctly.
* **Temporal Stability:** The lifter must stand still for a set duration (e.g., 2 seconds) to prove they are ready to begin the set. This prevents accidental calibration while setting up.

## 3. STATE TRANSITION
* **PENDING:** Searching for a visible, correctly positioned lifter.
* **CALIBRATING:** Lifter is visible and in the correct position. The system is waiting for them to remain still.
* **SUCCESS:** The lifter is stable and in the correct position. The system is now ready to begin counting reps.
