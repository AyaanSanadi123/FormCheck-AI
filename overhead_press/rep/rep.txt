# OVERHEAD PRESS REP LOGIC: STRATEGY & SCORING
# Version: 1.0

## 1. CORE METRICS
* **Vertical ROM:** % of Arm Length traveled from shoulder to lockout.
* **Bar Path:** Horizontal deviation from a straight vertical line.
* **Stability:** Torso angle to detect excessive leaning back.

## 2. SCORING RULES (Start at 100)

### Major Deductions (-10)
1.  **"LEAN_BACK"**: Torso angle deviates more than the threshold from vertical.
2.  **"ASYMMETRY"**: Left/Right Wrist Y-difference is too large.

### Minor Deductions (-5)
1.  **"INCOMPLETE_LOCKOUT"**: Elbows not fully extended at the top of the rep.
2.  **"SHALLOW_DEPTH"**: Bar does not return to the shoulder/clavicle area.
3.  **"BAD_PATH"**: Bar drifts too far horizontally during the press.

## 3. STATE MACHINE TRIGGERS
* **IDLE**: Bar is at the shoulders, waiting for upward movement.
* **ASCENDING**: Bar velocity is negative (moving up).
* **DESCENDING**: Bar velocity is positive (moving down).
* **COMPLETE**: Bar returns to the shoulder area.

## 4. DATA INPUTS
* `gatekeeper.calibration`: Contains `shoulder_y` (Shoulder Height) and `arm_length`.
* `normalizer.process`: Returns `aligned_landmarks` (Shoulder-Anchored).
