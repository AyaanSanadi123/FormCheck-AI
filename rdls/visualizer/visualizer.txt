================================================================
RDL VISUALIZER: HINGE & DRIFT UI
Version: 1.0 (Sagittal Plane Feedback)
================================================================

1. SPINAL PILLAR
----------------
Draws a high-contrast line between the Shoulder and Hip. 
Turns RED if lumbar flexion is detected, warning the lifter 
to brace their core and flatten their back.

2. HIP HINGE TRACKER
--------------------
Monitors the horizontal distance between the Hip and the 
Ankle. Provides a visual 'Stretch' indicator when the 
hip reaches the target depth in the -X direction.

3. BAR DRIFT GAUGE
------------------
A horizontal 'Tether' line connects the Wrist to the 
Shin vertical. If the gap exceeds the safety threshold, 
the line turns RED to indicate the load is too far 
from the center of mass.



4. SQUAT AVOIDANCE
------------------
Highlights the Knee-to-Ankle segment. If this segment 
tilts forward (knee driving over toes), the indicator 
warns of 'Squat-Cheating.'
