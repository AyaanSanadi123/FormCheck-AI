================================================================
NORMALIZER: ANKLE-ANCHORED HINGE SPACE
Version: 1.0 (Sagittal Plane Transformation)
================================================================

1. COMPONENT PURPOSE
--------------------
Standardizes the lifter's position so that the 'Hinge' is 
measured purely as horizontal (X) travel. This removes 
the bias of lifters standing at different depths or 
angles relative to the lens.

2. COORDINATE MAPPING
---------------------
* Ankle: (0, 0) - The fixed pivot of the lower body.
* Floor: Aligned to the X-axis (Y=0).
* Neutral Stand: Hip X ≈ 0, Shoulder X ≈ 0.
* The Hinge: Hip X moves in the negative direction (-X).
* Bar Path: Wrist X relative to Ankle X.

3. WHY THIS IS "GOLD STANDARD"
------------------------------
A. Tilt Cancellation: Even if the camera is held at an angle, 
   the rotation based on the foot-to-floor contact ensures 
   the spine's 'verticality' is measured accurately.
B. Dimensionless Units: By scaling by leg length, a 'good 
   hinge' is defined as Hip X < -0.15 (15% of leg length), 
   making the logic universal for all body types.
