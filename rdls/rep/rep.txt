1. THE "HINGE-FIRST" PROTOCOL
-----------------------------
Unlike a standard deadlift, the RDL rep begins with horizontal 
hip displacement. The 'IDLE' to 'ECCENTRIC' transition is 
triggered solely by the Hip X-coordinate.

2. CRITICAL FAULT DETECTION
---------------------------
* SQUAT_FAULT (-15): Detected if the knee angle deviates 
  from the 'Soft Knee' baseline calibrated in the Gatekeeper. 
  Indicates the user is offloading the hamstrings.
* LUMBAR_ROUND (-20): Triggered if the Shoulder-Hip-Knee angle 
  breaks linearity. This is a high-priority safety stop.
* BAR_DRIFT (-10): Measures the horizontal gap between the 
  Wrist and the Ankle. A drifting bar increases spinal shear.

3. SUCCESS CRITERIA
-------------------
A 'Clean Rep' requires a Hip X-displacement of at least 20% 
of the leg length. This ensures the lifter reaches the 
'Stretch' state where hamstring recruitment is highest.
