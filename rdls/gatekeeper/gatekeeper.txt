================================================================
RDL GATEKEEPER: POSTERIOR CHAIN STANDARDS
Version: 1.0 (Side-Profile Integrity)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
Ensures the camera is placed at a true 90-degree angle. This 
is mandatory to track the horizontal displacement of the hip 
relative to the ankle (the 'Hinge').

2. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS (check)
----------------------------------
A. Joint Stacking Baseline:
- Confirms the lifter starts in a neutral standing position 
  with the Hip-Shoulder-Ankle vertically aligned.

B. Knee Unlock Calibration:
- Measures the 'Starting Knee Angle.' In an RDL, the knees 
  should be 'soft' but not bent like a squat. This baseline 
  prevents 'Squat-Cheating' during the rep.

C. Lead-Side Visibility:
- Requires 90% visibility for the shoulder, hip, and ankle 
  to ensure the skeletal model is accurate for depth tracking.

3. CALIBRATION OUTPUTS
----------------------
* active_side: LEFT or RIGHT for tracking.
* scale_factor: Full leg length (Hip to Ankle) used to normalize 
  horizontal hip travel.
* base_knee_angle: The reference angle for 'soft knees.'
