# ONE ARM ROW VISUALIZER: STRATEGY & UX
# Version: 1.0
# Blueprint Standard: Compliant

## 1. THE CHALLENGE: "VISUALIZING INVISIBLE MECHANICS"
The user cannot see their back angle or the trajectory of the dumbbell while looking at the floor/bench. The visualizer must provide immediate, high-contrast feedback on these two specific mechanics: the Torso Heave and the J-Curve.

## 2. THE VISUAL SOLUTIONS

### A. The "Torso Metronome" (Back Angle)
* **Concept:** We draw a thick line connecting the active Hip to the active Shoulder. 
* **Visual:** * Green/White = Torso is stable and close to the original setup angle.
    * Flashing Red = "TORSO_HEAVE" fault. The line turns red and thickens, instantly warning the user that they are using their lower back to cheat the weight up.

### B. The "J-Curve Tracer" (Wrist Path)
* **Concept:** A trailing path history that tracks the wrist's exact X/Y coordinates in pixel space.
* **Visual:** As the user pulls, the line should naturally form a swooping "J" shape (pulling up and back towards the hip).
    * Cyan = Good path.
    * Yellow = "BICEP_PULL" (the line goes straight up and down vertically) or "SHORT_PULL" (the line doesn't go high enough).

### C. HUD & Standardization
* Follows the `PIPELINE_BLUEPRINT.md` color requirements: Green for passing, Orange/Yellow for minor ROM faults, Red for severe mechanical breakdowns.
