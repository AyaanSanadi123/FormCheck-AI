================================================================
PEC DECK VISUALIZER: RENDERING STANDARDS
Version: 1.1 (Bilateral Adduction UI)
================================================================

1. VISUAL FEEDBACK
------------------
* Midline Guide: Renders a vertical axis through the sternum to 
  help users visualize the "meeting point" for their wrists.
* Shoulder Stability: Joint pivots at landmarks 11/12 turn RED if 
  shrugging (Shoulder Hike) is detected.

2. HUD & TOASTS
---------------
* Displays Reps, Score, and current State (e.g., CONCENTRIC). 
* Feedback messages like "KEEP SHOULDERS DOWN" or "PEAK SQUEEZE" 
  are toasted at the bottom center.



3. COLOR STANDARDS
------------------
Adheres to PIPELINE_BLUEPRINT.md: 
- Green: Good form. 
- Red: Active faults. 
- Orange: Transition states or warnings.
