1. THE "HORIZONTAL SHOULDER" TRANSFORMATION
-----------------------------------------
This version rotates the skeletal frame so that the vector 
between Landmark 11 and 12 is perfectly parallel to the X-axis.

2. IMPACT ON ANALYSIS
---------------------
* Pivot Lock: The Sternum is the absolute (0,0).
* Symmetry: Left/Right adduction is measured from a level center.
* Scale: Normalized to the user's Bi-acromial width.

3. COORDINATE MAPPING
---------------------
* Sternum: (0, 0)
* Left Shoulder: (-0.5, 0)
* Right Shoulder: (0.5, 0)
* Wrists (Peak): Converge toward (0, y) at the end of Concentric.
