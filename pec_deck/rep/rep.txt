================================================================
PEC DECK REP LOGIC: BILATERAL STANDARDS
Version: 1.1 (Symmetry & Adduction Focus)
================================================================

1. PRIMARY METRIC
-----------------
* Inter-Wrist Distance: Measures the horizontal adduction convergence. 
  A valid rep requires the wrists to meet at a distance < 35% of 
  the shoulder width.
* Vertical Shoulder Stability: Monitors 'Shoulder Hiking' by 
  tracking Y-axis drift from the coronal-aligned baseline.

2. STATE MACHINE (Concentric-First)
-----------------------------------
1. IDLE: Arms open, ready to initiate.
2. CONCENTRIC: Hands/Elbows moving toward the midline.
3. TOP: Peak contraction reached at minimum distance.
4. ECCENTRIC: Controlled opening to the start position.
5. COMPLETE: Resets the cycle for the next rep.

3. FAULT DETECTION
------------------
* SHOULDER_HIKE (-15): Triggered if shoulders elevate during the 
  squeeze. Indicates traps are compensating for fatigued pecs.
* PARTIAL_ROM (-20): Triggered if the eccentric phase is cut short, 
  failing to reach the full stretch baseline (>90% shoulder width).



4. TECHNICAL NOTES
------------------
This logic benefits from the Coronal-Aligned Normalizer; the 
distance is calculated as a pure X-axis difference, making it 
immune to slight torso leans or tilts.
