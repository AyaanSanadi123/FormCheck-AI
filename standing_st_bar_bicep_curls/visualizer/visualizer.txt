================================================================
BICEP CURL VISUALIZER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
# Version: 1.0 (Sagittal Feedback HUD)
================================================================

1. THE "ELBOW ANCHOR" HUD
-------------------------
The primary visual cue is the Elbow Crosshair.
- GOAL: Provide a "target" for the user's elbow.
- BEHAVIOR: If the elbow drifts horizontally (X-axis) beyond the 
  calibrated threshold, the crosshair expands and turns RED.
- PURPOSE: Instant feedback that the user is "swinging" rather 
  than "curling."

2. THE "PLUMB LINE" (Spine Integrity)
-------------------------------------
A thick line is rendered between the Shoulder and Hip.
- GOAL: Highlight torso stability.
- BEHAVIOR: The line remains white as long as the spine is 
  within 10° of vertical. If the user leans back (using momentum), 
  the line turns RED and an AR arrow appears at the shoulder 
  pointing backwards.



3. DYNAMIC FLEXION GAUGE
------------------------
Numerical angle display positioned next to the elbow joint.
- ROM TARGET: The number turns GREEN once the user reaches the 
  "Deep Contraction" zone (< 45°).
- ROM WARNING: If the user stops at 90°, the number remains WHITE, 
  visually signaling an incomplete rep.

4. KINETIC HIGHLIGHTS
---------------------
- FOREARM VECTOR: A line between the Elbow and Wrist that 
  changes brightness based on velocity.
- TOAST NOTIFICATIONS: Large text at the bottom of the screen 
  providing cues like "PIN YOUR ELBOWS" or "LOWER SLOWLY."



NOTE: This profile-view visualizer is designed to make "cheating" 
visible. By emphasizing the static nature of the upper arm and 
torso, the user is forced to focus on the isolation of the 
biceps brachii.
