BICEP CURL REP LOGIC: STRATEGY & SCORING
# Version: 1.0 (Sagittal Flexion Logic)
================================================================

1. CORE METRICS
---------------
* Flexion Angle: The internal angle of the elbow joint.
* Elbow Drift Ratio: The horizontal displacement of the elbow 
  normalized by humerus length.
* Sagittal Lean: The angular deviation of the spine from the 
  vertical plumb line.

2. REFINED SCORING RULES (Start at 100)
---------------------------------------

Major Deductions (-15)
1. "ELBOW_SWAY": Occurs when the elbow moves >15% of humerus 
   length away from the starting anchor. This indicates the 
   shoulders are assisting the lift.
2. "PARTIAL_ROM": If the peak contraction fails to reach < 45°. 
   Usually caused by using weights that are too heavy.

Minor Deductions (-10)
1. "BACK_LEAN": Detected when the torso tilts backward >10° 
   to help pull the bar through the sticking point.
2. "CONTROL": A rapid extension phase (dropping the bar) 
   without resisting the weight.



3. FLEXION STATE MACHINE
------------------------
* FLEXING: Angle moves from 160° towards 45°. This is the 
  concentric phase.
* EXTENDING: Angle moves from 45° back towards 160°. This is 
  the eccentric phase.
* COMPLETE: Rep is counted only if the minimum angle achieved 
  meets the peak contraction threshold.

4. DATA INPUTS
--------------
* normalizer.landmarks: Provides the "Spine-Vertical" coordinates.
* gatekeeper.calibration: Provides the 'elbow_x_anchor' to 
  monitor for swing cheating.



NOTE: This logic specifically targets "Cheat Curls." By locking 
the elbow in 3D space, the system forces the user to rely on 
the biceps brachii rather than total body momentum.
