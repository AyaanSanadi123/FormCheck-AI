================================================================
BICEP CURL GATEKEEPER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
Version: 1.0 (Sagittal Profile Calibration)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
The Gatekeeper establishes a "Side-Profile" baseline. Its primary 
task is to map the user's arm levers (Humerus and Forearm) and 
lock the horizontal "Elbow Anchor." This allows the system to 
measure flexion without being fooled by torso swaying or 
shoulder swinging.

2. THE VALIDATION PIPELINE
--------------------------

A. Frame-Level Rejection (Instant)
- PROFILE VERIFICATION: The Z-depth difference between the Left 
  and Right shoulders must be > 0.25. This ensures the user is 
  truly perpendicular to the lens, providing a clear view of the 
  elbow joint.
- VISIBILITY: Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, and Hip of the side closest 
  to the camera must have confidence > 0.90.
- INITIAL STATE: The Wrist must be vertically lower than the 
  Elbow. Calibration will not trigger if the user starts with 
  the bar mid-curl.



B. Temporal Validation (Stability Window)
- WINDOW SIZE: 45 Frames (~1.5 seconds).
- REQUIREMENT: The "Shoulder-to-Hip" alignment must remain nearly 
  vertical. This ensures the user isn't starting in a leaned-back 
  posture which would skew the "Cheating" thresholds.

3. THE CALIBRATION PASSPORT (Data Latch)
----------------------------------------
Upon successful validation, the following anatomical data is locked:

* humerus_baseline: The distance from Shoulder to Elbow. 
  - USE: Acts as the "Pivot Radius."
* forearm_baseline: The distance from Elbow to Wrist. 
  - USE: Acts as the "Lever Length" for calculating torque speed.
* elbow_x_anchor: The neutral horizontal position of the elbow.
  - USE: If the elbow drifts > 15% of humerus length during a 
    rep, it is flagged as "ELBOW_SWAY."
* neutral_spine_v: The initial vector from Hip to Shoulder.
  - USE: The baseline for detecting "BACK_LEAN" momentum.



4. USER FEEDBACK STRATEGY
-------------------------
* "Turn 90 degrees to face the side": Triggered if the user faces 
  the camera directly.
* "Ensure your side profile is visible": Triggered by limb dropout.
* "Calibrating Side Profile...": Visual indicator as the system 
  locks the elbow anchor.



NOTE: By isolating the 'humerus_baseline' and 'forearm_baseline' 
separately, the analyzer can detect "partial range of motion" 
failures more accurately than a simple height-based check.
