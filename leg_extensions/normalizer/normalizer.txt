SEATED LEG EXTENSION NORMALIZER: STANDARDIZATION LOGIC
Version: 1.0 (Knee-Origin Canonical Pose)
================================================================

1. COMPONENT PURPOSE
------------------------------
Standardizes raw MediaPipe output into a coordinate system where 
the Knee is the origin (0,0) and the user's thigh length equals 1.0. 
This ensures the Rep Logic math remains constant regardless of 
camera distance or user height.

2. STANDARDIZATION RULES
----------------------------------
A. Origin (Translation):
- The Knee (ID 25/26) is the primary mechanical pivot for this 
  exercise. It is moved to (0,0) to simplify angular and 
  velocity calculations of the lower leg.

B. Facing (Reflection):
- If the user is facing LEFT (detected by the Gatekeeper), the 
  X-coordinates are inverted. 
- RESULT: In the normalized space, the foot always kicks toward 
  the POSITIVE X-axis.

C. Scale (Normalization):
- All coordinates are divided by the 'scale_factor' (Baseline 
  Thigh Length).
- RESULT: The Hip will always be located at approximately (-1, 0) 
  when the thigh is horizontal, providing a consistent lever 
  reference.



3. IMPACT ON PIPELINE
-------------------------------
By rectifying the coordinate system here, the Rep Logic can 
use hard-coded thresholds (e.g., "Ankle Y must reach > 0.8") 
without needing to know the user's physical height or the 
camera's zoom level.
