================================================================
HAMMER CURL VISUALIZER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
Version: 1.0 (The Hammer Handle HUD)
================================================================

1. THE "HAMMER HANDLE" OVERLAY
------------------------------
The visualizer renders a thick, high-contrast line over the 
forearm (Elbow to Wrist).
- CYAN: Correct neutral grip.
- RED: Supination detected (Wrist is rotating).

2. THE PIVOT CROSSHAIR
----------------------
A magenta crosshair is locked onto the elbow.
- BEHAVIOR: If the elbow moves out of the "Drift Zone," the 
  crosshair pulses red and displays the "KEEP ELBOWS FIXED" 
  warning.



3. FLEXION GAUGE
----------------
A numerical degree readout follows the wrist. It turns green 
only when the "Peak Zone" (50°) is achieved, encouraging the 
user to finish the range of motion.
