HAMMER CURL REP LOGIC: STRATEGY & SCORING
# Version: 1.1 (Neutral Axis & Safety Clipping)
================================================================

1. CORE METRICS
---------------
* Brachial Flexion: Internal elbow angle. Unlike standard curls, 
  we look for a slightly shallower peak (50°) due to the 
  mechanical stop of the forearm in a neutral position.
* Grip Rotation (Supination): The Z-depth variance between the 
  Thumb (21) and Pinky (18). 
* Elbow Anchor Drift: Horizontal (X) displacement of the elbow 
  joint relative to the humerus length.

2. REFINED SCORING RULES (Start at 100)
---------------------------------------

Major Deductions (-15)
1. "ELBOW_SWAY": Triggered if the elbow drifts > 12% of the 
   humerus length. This indicates momentum assisted by the 
   anterior deltoid.
2. "GRIP_ROTATE": Triggered if the Z-variance between finger 
   landmarks exceeds 0.08. This catches the user turning the 
   palms upward (supinating) to engage the biceps brachii.



Minor Deductions (-10)
1. "PARTIAL_ROM": If the user fails to descend past 150° 
   (Incomplete extension) or ascend past 50° (Short-changing 
   the contraction).

3. STATE MACHINE & SAFETY
-------------------------
* IDLE: Resting at bottom (> 150°).
* CURLING: Concentric phase. System applies `np.clip` to 
  dot products to prevent floating-point arccos errors.
* DESCENDING: Eccentric phase. Score is finalized once 
  extension exceeds the reset threshold.



4. DATA INTEGRITY (Safety Checks)
---------------------------------
- Zero-Norm Protection: If landmarks overlap or visibility 
  drops, the angle defaults to 180° to prevent NaN crashes.
- Division Safety: The 'humerus_baseline' is hard-coded to 
  1.0 if the Gatekeeper fails, preventing DivisionByZero errors.



NOTE: Version 1.1 focuses on isolating the Brachialis. By 
penalizing grip rotation, we ensure the hammer-specific 
loading is maintained throughout the entire range of motion.
