1. THE CORE OBJECTIVE: "SQUARING THE SAGITTAL PLANE"
----------------------------------------------------
To detect "Cheating" via back-leaning, the skeleton must be 
normalized so that the spine is perfectly vertical at the start 
of the exercise. 

2. REFINED ROTATION LOGIC
-------------------------
A. Hip-Centric Pivot:
- The rotation is pinned to the Hip (24). This allows the 
  analyzer to measure "Torso Sway" as an angular deviation 
  from a fixed base.



B. X-Y Plane Correction:
- The Normalizer calculates the current lean angle of the 
  Shoulder-to-Hip vector and applies an inverse rotation to 
  force that vector to 180° (Straight Up).
- RESULT: Any movement of the shoulder backward during the 
  curl is now a mathematically pure "Lean" relative to gravity.
