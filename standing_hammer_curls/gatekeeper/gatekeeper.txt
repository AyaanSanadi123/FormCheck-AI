HAMMER CURL GATEKEEPER: OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY
Version: 1.0 (Neutral Grip & Profile Alignment)
================================================================

1. CORE OBJECTIVE
-----------------
The Hammer Curl requires a specific "Neutral Grip" where palms face 
the torso. The Gatekeeper's primary job is to verify this grip 
orientation and establish a stable side-profile anchor before 
allowing the rep counter to engage.

2. THE VALIDATION PIPELINE
--------------------------
A. Grip Verification (The Hammer Check):
- Monitors the Z-depth of the Index Finger (20) relative to the 
  Wrist (16). In a neutral grip, these landmarks should align 
  on the same vertical plane in a side profile.
- If the thumb is significantly closer to the camera than the 
  wrist, the user is likely supinating (Standard Curl).

B. Profile Depth:
- Z-depth difference between shoulders must be > 0.25 to ensure 
  the camera has a clear view of the elbow pivot.



3. THE CALIBRATION PASSPORT
---------------------------
* humerus_baseline: The upper arm length used to set the pivot radius.
* elbow_x_anchor: The fixed horizontal coordinate for the elbow.
* neutral_grip_z: The baseline Z-depth for rotation detection.
